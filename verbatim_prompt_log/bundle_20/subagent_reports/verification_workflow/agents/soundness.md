# verify:soundness

## Verdict

Not approvable as written. 1 blocker and 4 majors remain, plus 6 minors. What the v2 fixes achieved: soundness F1 (held sets) is closed by FZ-4 and Appendix B's held sets, which use the elaborated closure: other cards' statements and proofs, instances and coercions. Implementability F1 (the instance ban) is closed by dropping the ban in favour of the meaning comparison. Soundness F2 and logic F1 are closed for every change that touches a `.holds`, through the name-and-type new-axiom rule and RV-7's axiom bullet. Only a `type:`-line-only kind change is still unenforced (minor, F6). Of the v2 majors, these are closed: the preset (ES-2, applied to every inference), data telelean keeps (CF-3, apart from `type:`, origin and session), proposed premises (RV-1; details of the completion are minor, F7) and transform proofs (TR-14, with copied Proof fields frozen and never fuzzy). Two are not. Meaning-before-proof is still open: a separate Prove item still reveals the outcome (F2). The gate-baseline fix opened a new hole: "what Claude-free transforms produced" lets Claude's copied content count as yours (F1). Problems in the new mechanisms: TR-15 inside a session, combined with DO-2, turns Claude's proposals into accepted items without the Accept each belief needs (blocker). The Apply backstop in §11.5 contradicts what RV-1 and RV-7 allow to be accepted as a group (F3). Allow this change conflicts with the frozen check, and a meaning change you rejected can still reach your files (F5). Claude's rewording of a formalised card's sentence can be group-accepted (F4). On RV-11 (a proof you accepted counts as your own), I found no circularity and no widening of the permitted set of its own. Its dependencies are accepted and frozen. The circular-footprint drop still applies. Lean's declaration order rules out cycles. RV-11's only exposure is through F1.

## Findings

### F1 [blocker] — TR-15 (inside a session), DO-2, RV-8, RV-7 last line, TR-4, Appendix B (Baseline, Backstop), §11.4, §11.5, §11.6

**Problem.** A transform that needs no Claude, done inside a session, turns Claude's proposed content into "already accepted" items, and into your baseline. DO-2 opens the transform window on Claude's proposed version, and TR-4 copies its Lean statement into the draft as frozen. So a Decline → Transform in which you edit only prose has no fuzzy field, no instructions and no open Lean field. TR-15 then applies it at Submit review "as items already accepted". Appendix B's baseline then adds "what Claude-free transforms produced", so Claude's text becomes your text, which contradicts "Claude's content never counts as your text". Scenario (Spyro, These suffice): the stage shows `def InGame (c) (g) : Prop := GoodAddition c g` (a belief item) and Formalize ‹PG games cannot have characters with dark pasts› = `∀ g c, IsPG g → HasDarkPast c → ¬ InGame c g` (a belief item). You select both and choose Decline → Transform (RV-8's multi-card transform). You fix a typo in the premise's Why and submit. Both items become accepted: neither gets its own Accept, RV-5's order is skipped, and the Result is revealed (RV-6). That is S5's definition cheat, adopted through a decline. Whether §11.5's backstop stops it depends on bookkeeping the spec never states: does "accepted on its own" include a TR-15 acceptance? Because of the baseline rule, InGame and P's `.holds` count as yours. A pending Claude proof carried through such a transform, or rewritten by a D-11 follow-on edit, would count as your own proof and widen later inferences' permitted sets.

**Suggested fix.** TR-15, replace the inside-a-session bullet with: "inside a session, only what you typed and telelean's follow-on edits count as yours (RV-7). Content the window copied from Claude's proposal (DO-2, or any card with a pending item) stays Claude's. Its item keeps its id and its RV-7 class, and returns to 'in review', marked 'updated' if you changed any of its fields. Belief items and items that change what is proved still need their own Accept, and RV-5 and RV-6 apply." DO-2, add: "Fields copied from Claude's proposal are marked 'Claude's proposal'. Submitting the window does not accept them." Appendix B, Baseline, replace the first bullet with: "your files at session start, plus what you typed and telelean's follow-on edits from your transforms, committed with Lean facts rebuilt. Content copied from Claude's proposal never joins the baseline and never counts as your proof."

### F2 [major] — RV-1 (Prove, Result, 'at most two items'), RV-2, RV-6, §11.6, S7

**Problem.** The meaning-before-proof fix does not hold. RV-1 keeps a separate item, "Prove ‹card›", which "adds or replaces a proof, and lists what it rests on". It exists only when a proof was produced. In These suffice only targets get proofs, so the list shows "Prove ‹goal›", with its rests-on list, a 'depends on N' badge and the make-it-a-Derivation tick, exactly when the goal was proved. RV-6 hides only the Result item, the report and the Proposed view's chips. It does not hide the Prove item, or the target's Proof field in the Proposed view or in item details. Scenario: the list shows Formalize ‹PG games…› (belief), def InGame (belief), Prove ‹Cynder would not be a good addition› and a neutral Result. You judge InGame already knowing that it makes the goal proved, which is the automation bias that D-19 and §11.6 exist to prevent. The target also ends up with three items (fields, Prove, Result), against RV-1's 'at most two'.

**Suggested fix.** RV-1: "A target's proof belongs to its Result item; a target has no separate Prove item. Prove exists only for a card that is not the target of an inference, and These suffice never produces one. Result ‹target› carries the proof, what it rests on, 'replaces your proof' and 'make it a Derivation', all hidden until revealed." RV-6, add to the hidden list: "whether a proof exists: the target's Proof field and proof status in the Proposed view and in every item's detail." §11.6, add: "A premise Claude proposes ('closes the gap') necessarily shows that the goal did not follow from your premises alone."

### F3 [major] — §11.5, Appendix B (Backstop at Apply), RV-7 bullet 4, RV-1 (Vocabulary (N)), ES-7, EN-2 (reuse), ★D2 cost

**Problem.** As written, the backstop contradicts what RV-1 and RV-7 allow to be accepted as a group. "Every axiom, definition and instance that is new or changed since the session started must belong to an item you accepted on its own" covers all of these: new sorts and predicates (`axiom Character : Type` and `axiom HasDarkPast : Character → Prop` are axioms), which sit in the group-acceptable Vocabulary (N); the presupposition instance `instance : Nonempty T := T.exists`, for which RV-1 defines no item; proof-only definitions and instances, which RV-7 does not count as beliefs; and your own hand edits (ES-7), which are not items at all. Read literally, it refuses Apply in every session that formalises from scratch. That is v2's instance-ban blocker again, in a new place. Relaxed to 'any accepted item', it no longer checks RV-7's classification, which was its whole purpose. RV-7's bullet "adds or changes a definition, instance or vocabulary item that a statement uses" is ambiguous in the same way. Read literally, every new predicate a statement uses is a belief item, which contradicts RV-1 and the cost of 10–12 clicks you are asked to approve in ★D2. "Uses" is also not defined as the elaborated closure. An instance found by type-class search, which today's code never flags, could therefore be classed as proof-only. Finally, EN-2 may reuse "the earlier result" at Apply, which could skip the backstop. That reuse is unsafe here because the backstop depends on item statuses, not only on the files.

**Suggested fix.** Appendix B and §11.5: "At every Apply (never reused), telelean lists each declaration of the final state that is new or changed relative to the session base plus your own edits. Each must belong to an item you accepted individually: (a) every axiom except new sorts, predicates, constants and functions that pass the shape rule; (b) every definition, instance or coercion in the elaborated closure of any card's Lean statement, with the closure computed as for held sets; (c) every existing name whose kind, type or value changed. Also exempt: the presupposition instance of an accepted presupposition, lemmas, and definitions or instances that no statement reaches. Otherwise Apply is refused, naming the declaration." RV-7, bullet 4: "adds a definition, instance or coercion, or changes any existing vocabulary item, that is in the elaborated closure of a card's Lean statement. New vocabulary that only declares a sort, predicate, constant or function and passes the shape rule is not a belief." RV-1: "The presupposition instance belongs to its presupposition's item. Any other new instance gets its own item."

### F4 [major] — RV-7 ('changes what is proved'), RV-4, TR-4, TR-13, CF-6

**Problem.** When Claude rewords the sentence of a formalised DERIVATION or GOAL, the result is a plain Change that can be group-accepted. Yet the sentence is what 'proved' is shown under. RV-7 counts only changes to Lean as 'changes what is proved', and TR-13's mismatch warning covers only your own sentence edits. Scenario: you run Transform cards… on the goal with the instruction "tidy the wording". TR-4 copies its Lean `¬ GoodAddition cynder newSpyro` as frozen and its sentence as open. Claude writes "Cynder would not be a good addition to any Spyro game". The Change ‹goal› item is accepted together with others in a group. Once the goal is proved, now or by a mid-stage These suffice, the board shows 'proved' under a sentence the Lean does not say, and nobody judged the new sentence against the Lean.

**Suggested fix.** RV-7, 'changes what is proved', add: "or changes the sentence of any card that has a Lean statement. The item shows the new sentence above the unchanged Lean, as a meaning check, marked 'sentence and Lean may disagree', as TR-13 does for your own edits."

### F5 [major] — FZ-5 (Allow this change), FZ-3, RV-5, DO-1, Appendix B (meaning comparison, Backstop), §11.3

**Problem.** Allow this change does not fit the frozen check, and it lets a meaning change you rejected reach your files. (1) The allowed item X stays held, and its frozen term is never re-taken: FZ-3 takes frozen content only from your files and your own changes. So Apply's rule, "every frozen and held declaration still elaborates to its frozen term", refuses every session that used Allow. If the meaning comparison checks closures ('like G4's'), the frozen holders also fail every check during the session. (2) A holder's meaning change cannot be detected from its own term: `abbrev cynder_dark_past : Prop := HasDarkPast cynder` elaborates to the same term after HasDarkPast's body changes. (3) Nothing ties X to its 'meaning changed' items. Under RV-5 a meaning-changed item depends on X, but X does not depend on it. Scenario: the preset makes ‹Cynder has a dark past› hold HasDarkPast. You choose Allow. Claude writes `def HasDarkPast (c : Character) : Prop := ¬ GoodAddition c newSpyro`. You accept Change HasDarkPast as a refinement, then Reject 'meaning changed: Cynder has a dark past'. DO-1 has nothing to restore, because the text never changed. X stays accepted and every item is decided. If Apply exempts allowed items, as it must to work at all given (1), the assumption's new meaning reaches your files even though you rejected it.

**Suggested fix.** FZ-5, replace the held-content bullet with: "Allow this change on X: X is exempt from the held check, and frozen holders are compared with X's new version in place of the old. Every frozen card whose held closure contains X gets a 'meaning changed by X' item; membership of the closure decides this, not a comparison of the card's own term. X's item can be accepted only after each of these items is accepted, individually where RV-7 requires it. Declining any of them declines X and restores X. Accepting X makes its accepted version X's frozen term." §11.3: "… except held content you allowed to change (FZ-5), whose consequences you accept one by one." Appendix B, Backstop, add: "No allowed item has changed unless it and all its 'meaning changed' items are accepted."

### F6 [minor] — CF-3, CF-4, TR-4, TR-7, DO-3, Appendix B (Replaced)

**Problem.** Two promises have no mechanism behind them: 'Claude never changes the Kind of an existing card' and 'must stick to the cards in the window'. Kind (`type:`) is not in CF-3's list of data Claude never changes. TR-4 gives Kind no starting state. Appendix B's replacement for G5 has no rule against new blocks. A change to the `type:` line alone adds no axiom, so neither RV-7 nor the backstop sees it, and it can be group-accepted on a transformed or Shaky card. Examples: GOAL → ASSUMPTION before any `.holds` exists, after which a later These suffice writes `.holds` for 'your' assumption; or ASSUMPTION → DERIVATION, which re-files a belief as a claimed derivation. A kind change that also touches `.holds` is caught by the name-and-type new-axiom rule and by RV-7's axiom bullet.

**Suggested fix.** CF-3, add to the list: "its Kind (`type:`), origin and session". Appendix B, Replaced, add: "Claude's working copy may not change an existing block's `type:`. It may add a block only when 'Claude may add cards' is ticked, for a Shaky split, or as new vocabulary; any other new block is refused."

### F7 [minor] — RV-1 (Add: proposed premise, mechanical completion), RV-6, RV-11

**Problem.** Three gaps in the mechanical completion of a proof. (a) The completed state is not said to be checked before the Result can show 'proved'. Only Apply's final check catches a failure, and then no Claude step remains to fix it. (b) The meaning you accepted for N is the one from the scratch check. telelean writes N's abbrev in a different place, before the target's block, where opens, local instances or later declarations can make the same text elaborate differently or not at all. The scratch proof also names `telelean_ghost_stmt`, so composing the proof means elaborating it again. (c) If you accept two proposed premises for one target, which completion stands is undefined.

**Suggested fix.** RV-1 Add, replace the completion sentence with: "On Accept, telelean writes N, re-elaborates it in place, and requires its fully explicit form to equal the one you accepted; otherwise the item returns to 'in review', marked 'meaning differs in place'. telelean then composes T.holds from the checked conditional proof and checks that state as it would a step: build, footprint with N selected, statements fixed and G4 for T. Until that passes, the Result reads 'completion not checked'. If you accept a second proposed premise for the same target, the first completion stays and the second premise is listed as unused."

### F8 [minor] — ES-2 (target's Proof open, 'stays exactly as it was'), FZ-4, Appendix B (Kept/Replaced), S1

**Problem.** (1) The spec gives no precedence between held and open. If another frozen proof uses `T.holds` (common when you re-prove a derivation from new premises), FZ-4 holds T's proof while ES-2 opens it. If open wins, the frozen card's footprint changes without an item for that card. (2) The rule "In a step that does not end in a proof, it stays exactly as it was" is what S1 cites to say report §3.7 item 7a is closed, but Appendix B has no rule that enforces it. existing-changed, which exempted T.holds anyway, is replaced by the frozen check. The frozen check does not cover an open field, and today's status-mismatch rule does not catch a deletion.

**Suggested fix.** ES-2, add: "Held beats open: if a frozen proof uses the target's proof, replacing it needs Allow this change (FZ-5), and the Result lists the cards whose footprint changes." Appendix B, Kept, add: "In a step that does not finish proved, each target's `.holds` must be byte-identical to its version at the step's start (status-mismatch extended to removal and to a changed theorem)."

### F9 [minor] — TR-15 (outside a session), TR-12, TR-13

**Problem.** Outside a session, TR-15's "no Lean check before writing" contradicts TR-12, which elaborates typed Lean in a scratch file. If TR-12 is skipped as a whole, S10's injection (`True`, a newline, then `axiom evil : False`) is written straight into your files. TR-15's test for needing Claude also misses two cases: typed Lean that names something not yet defined (TR-12's 'Claude must define: …'), and TR-13's choice 'leave the unfrozen ones to Claude'. Both are therefore applied without Claude and leave broken Lean behind.

**Suggested fix.** TR-15: "A transform needs Claude if it has a fuzzy field, instructions, 'Claude may add cards' ticked, an open Lean field on a card whose sentence you changed, typed Lean that names something not yet defined, or dependents you left to Claude (TR-13). Otherwise telelean applies it. Outside a session, it does so after TR-12's single-term rule, shape rule and scratch check, with no build: one Undo step, no session."

### F10 [minor] — RV-8, RV-5, §11.4, FZ-1, TR-15 (follow-on edits), ES-7

**Problem.** §11.4 (the accepted items always form a self-contained whole) can fail in two ways. First, RV-8 accepts selected items "whose dependencies are accepted or in the selection", while skipping the RV-7 items in that same selection. A plain item can therefore be accepted while a belief item it depends on stays in review. Second, RV-5 lists the ways an item leaves 'accepted', but omits your own change, in a transform, to accepted content or its dependencies (FZ-1 lets you change frozen fields), and telelean's D-11 follow-on rewrites. ES-7 covers only hand edits.

**Suggested fix.** RV-8: "… every selected item whose dependencies are accepted, or are accepted by this same action (RV-7 items never are)." RV-5, add: "An item also leaves 'accepted' when you change its content or anything it depends on: in a transform, by a D-11 follow-on edit, or by a hand edit (ES-7)."

### F11 [minor] — §11.2

**Problem.** The list in §11.2 is not the permitted set the mechanism actually uses, so 'resting on exactly what it lists' is false at the edges. 'Plain vocabulary' really means every non-Prop axiom that no card owns. That includes your own items that break the shape rule (TL111) and axioms in the library root, for example a constant `axiom c : {g : Game // IsPG g}`, which carries IsPG. Also, a selected DERIVATION or GOAL that is only claimed (its `.holds` is an axiom) contributes that unproved claim, which 'your selected premises' does not convey.

**Suggested fix.** §11.2, replace the first and fourth list items with: "your selected premises' `.holds`, including the claim of a selected claimed derivation or goal, named 'claimed'"; and "the signature: every non-Prop axiom that no card owns, including any of yours that break the shape rule or sit in the library root. Each one that is not a plain sort, predicate, function or constant is named in the report."
