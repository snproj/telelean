# review:soundness

## Verdict

Through the soundness lens the revision has the right shape. It freezes by default for the session, keeps the freeze store out of Claude's reach, makes acceptance dependency-aware, treats definitions and presuppositions as belief items, and reruns every check on exactly the final state before Apply. (g), an ungated state reaching the user's files, looks closed by EN-2/EN-3, provided the final check runs per inference with G4. But two core clauses are unsafe as written. First, FZ-5's held set ('vocabulary and definitions', computed from `uses`) leaves out other cards' statements and proofs and every instance or coercion Lean inserts. So a frozen assumption's meaning, even its consistency, can change through a group-accepted change to another card (F1). Second, RV-7's belief list leaves out every way an existing name becomes an axiom: proof to axiom, lemma to axiom, a kind change to ASSUMPTION, a changed presupposition. Today's new-axiom rule skips any name known in B, so once existing-changed is narrowed to frozen content these pass the gate and can be group-accepted, and §11.5 is false (F2). Several 'same as today' claims fail field by field. ES-2/★D3 freezes the goal's own proof, which breaks D-11 promotion and re-proving, and leaves telelean-kept fields and bindings unprotected, which reopens the pin re-pointing attack (F4, F5). RV-5 is weaker than D-19 and leaks results (F3). RV-4's 'self-contained whole' does not hold (F6). Gap-filling premises contradict 'every check runs as today' (F7). The meaning checks compare printed text and are skipped after drift (F9). And the gate's baseline during a session is never defined (F12). The Lean examples of S2 (`cynder_dark_past.holds.elim` does typecheck once HasDarkPast := False) and of S5 (InGame) are correct. S3, S9, S12 and S13 hold. S1, S2, S4, S6, S7, S8, S10 and S11 claim more than their fixes deliver. I recommend fixing F1 and F2, and adding EN-2's mechanical backstops, before the user approves ★D2/★D3. The backstops: the final state's axioms and definitions are exactly the session start's plus the belief items accepted individually, and every frozen and held declaration is term-identical to its frozen copy.

## Findings

### F1 [blocker] (safety) — FZ-5, RF-5, RV-1, TR-14, §17 (held sets), §11.3, §11.5

**Problem.** FZ-5 holds only 'the vocabulary and definitions' a frozen card's Lean uses, and §17 computes that from `uses`. In code, `lean.uses` records vocabulary items only: it walks through other cards' statement abbrevs without recording them. Its `refs` are `.ilean` source usages, which never include instances or coercions found by elaboration. So a frozen card's meaning can change through another card's statement. Scenario (Spyro, ES-3): the user holds a frozen ASSUMPTION 'If the new Spyro game is PG, Cynder would not be a good addition', Lean `abbrev pg_rules_out_cynder : Prop := new_spyro_pg → cynder_not_good` plus `axiom pg_rules_out_cynder.holds`. They start Transform cards… on the GOAL 'Cynder would not be a good addition' to reword it, so its copied Lean is open (TR-4). Claude changes `cynder_not_good` to `¬ GoodAddition cynder newSpyro ∧ ¬ IsPG newSpyro`. The frozen assumption's text, its fully explicit print (`Spyro.new_spyro_pg → Spyro.cynder_not_good`) and its `uses` set are all unchanged, so every frozen and held check passes. Yet the model now assumes `IsPG newSpyro → ¬ IsPG newSpyro`, which together with `new_spyro_pg.holds` is inconsistent, so every goal becomes provable from selected premises. 'Change ‹goal›' is not a belief item (RV-7), and RV-1 lists affected cards only for vocabulary cards, so a group Accept applies it. The same gap does two more things. When Claude rewrites the proof of a DERIVATION that an accepted or frozen proof uses (the DERIVATION being open in a transform), that proof's 'proved from' list changes. And an existing instance used by a frozen statement can have its body changed. TR-14 already assumes frozen cards that rely on a card 'would block Claude', but FZ-5 does not make it so.

**Evidence.** packages/core/src/project/build.ts:494 `if (item !== undefined && !uses.includes(item)) uses.push(item);` with `throughNode` (496) only enqueueing card statements; packages/core/src/facts.ts:149 'Usages inside this declaration (`.ilean` usages …)'; FZ-5 'the vocabulary and definitions its statement or proof uses'; RV-1 'For a vocabulary card, it lists the cards whose meaning changes with it'; TR-14 'says which are frozen, since those would block Claude'.

**Suggested fix.** FZ-5, first bullet, replace with: 'every declaration a frozen card's Lean mentions, directly or through what those mention: other cards' statements, `.holds` declarations and proofs, lemmas, definitions, vocabulary, and the instances and coercions Lean inserted. telelean computes this from Lean's elaborated terms (the constants `#print axioms` walks), not from source references.' RV-1, Change, add: 'Every Change item lists every card whose statement or proof mentions what it changes, frozen ones included; if any of them is an ASSUMPTION, the item is a belief item (RV-7).' §17: replace 'Held sets come from Lean's dependency facts (uses, references)' with 'Held sets are the transitive closure of the constants in each frozen declaration's elaborated type and value.'

### F2 [blocker] (safety) — RV-7, RV-1, CF-4, TR-4, TR-12, ES-4, EN-2, §11.5, S6

**Problem.** RV-7's list misses every way an existing name becomes an axiom. Today only `existing-changed` stops those, and the revision narrows it to frozen or held content. `new-axiom` skips any axiom whose name is known in B (by name, any kind). `vocabulary-shape` skips known names and unmarked axioms. `footprint` never judges axioms. Scenario (ES-3): the user transforms the proved DERIVATION 'Cynder would not be a good addition' and asks Claude to simplify it; its Proof field is open (TR-4). Claude replaces `theorem cynder_not_good.holds : cynder_not_good := pg_no_dark_past.holds newSpyro cynder new_spyro_pg.holds cynder_dark_past.holds` with `axiom cynder_not_good.holds : cynder_not_good`. G0, new-axiom (the name is known), footprint, vocabulary-shape and the markup rule all pass, and the card becomes 'claimed'. The item is a plain Change, not a belief item, so a group Accept applies it, and every derivation that used it now rests on an axiom Claude wrote. The same route works for three more changes: a lemma `theorem helper : P` turned into `axiom helper : P` (a TL104 hidden assumption); a Kind change GOAL/DERIVATION→ASSUMPTION proposed by Claude (Kind is an open field under TR-4, and TR-12 covers only kind changes the user makes); and a changed presupposition (RV-7 lists only 'adding' one). §11.5 'Nothing becomes something the model assumes without your individual Accept' is false as written.

**Evidence.** packages/core/src/gate/rules.ts:158 `const inBase = (name: string) => B.known.has(name);` and :309 `if (inBase(name) || allowedHolds.has(name) || why === undefined) continue;`; decls.ts:54 `known` = every parsed name plus every facts name; rules.ts:603 vocabulary-shape `if (inBase(item.name) || item.kind === 'unmarked-axiom') continue;`; gate-accept.md §2 footprint 'judged = … every named declaration added or changed since B of any kind except axiom'.

**Suggested fix.** RV-7, first bullet, replace with: 'A belief item is any item after which the model assumes something it did not assume before, or assumes it differently. It adds an ASSUMPTION card; or it adds or changes a Prop-valued axiom under any name (an ASSUMPTION's statement or `.holds`, a presupposition, an unmarked axiom, a claim `axiom D.holds` in place of a proof); or it adds or changes a definition, instance or vocabulary item that an ASSUMPTION's statement uses.' CF-4, add: 'Claude never changes the Kind of an existing card and never replaces a proof with an axiom. It may ask you to, like an unfreeze request, and telelean then applies D-11 (TR-12).' ### F4 [major] (inconsistency) — ES-2, ★D3, S1, §16, FZ-2

### F11 [major] (safety) — TR-4, RV-9, RV-7, RV-2, ES-2, S11
