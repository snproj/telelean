# review-completeness

## Summary
The traceability is sound at the ID level. I segmented prompt1.md myself (55 sentences) and confirmed by script that every Appendix A row appears verbatim and in order. t1's validator re-runs with no errors. Checked against the design itself, not just the IDs, 49 of 55 sentences have a concrete design element: SelectionTray (S26), the overlay table plus vscode.changes (S31), D-17's drag rules (S16), the §2 node model (S34), D-28 plus a versioned export (S43), the themable formalizedAura (S41) and §10/§12 (S52). Six are weak: S15 (arrow meaning), S32 (new vocabulary hidden in a collapsed group), S44 (learnharness lessons not surfaced), S48 (Step-2 coverage) and S54/S55 (logging). None is missing outright. The files honour M2–M4. M1 is honoured during development but not yet at runtime. 03 was revised at 02:55 while I was reviewing (§8.2 Packaging, §12 run directions), and this audit covers that version.

The real gaps are unspoken constraints with large design impact that 02 never raises, plus some contradictions. (1) As written, telelean activates in every Lean project you open, including Mathlib or pre-4.35 ones, and runs an uncapped `lake build` plus the extractor on save. `lake comparator` and `leanexport` exist only in v4.35.0 (verified). Given your RAM-crash history, this is the top risk. (2) Nothing beyond add and kind-change is defined: no delete/retract, merge or supersede, though your cynder.lean uses supersede. Gate G5 also forbids agent deletions and reclassifications that the overlay, IC-10 and Instruct assume are possible. (3) There is no notion of several subjects per workspace, though your sketches keep five in one folder. A name clash between subjects breaks the root import that telelean maintains (verified). (4) There is no board-level undo or model history. Git snapshots only happen if a repo already exists, and the scaffold never creates one. A mistaken D→A drag deletes a proof. (5) Doc 01 still states the logging rule that M3/M4 replaced, and nobody checked the spectec example.

The Step-3 deliverable is close to actionable: a no-sudo Node install with checksum, an exact Lean pin, only the optional xvfb needs sudo, and installing from the repo folder solves the SDK-binary problem. The remaining gaps are minor: some pins are not exact, @types/react(-dom) and ajv are missing, two test downloads are unlisted, and the claim that your existing login is reused is unverified, with no auth preflight.

- **CMP-02** [major] doc all § 03 §6 EditService ('delete'), §7 ProposalBanner/overlay row 'removed', §8.5 G5; 02 D-17, D-27 G5; 01 IC-10

## Questions the docs should ask the user
- Should one Lean project hold one mental model (subject) or several, as in your telelean_scripts folder (bank, bayonetta, cynder, kopiteh, smoking)? Do you want relations across subjects, like bayonetta's 'contrast cynder.goal'?
- Should telelean switch itself on in all your Lean projects, including Mathlib ones, or only in folders you initialise as telelean models?
- What should deleting an assumption do: remove it and flag what breaks, or 'suspend' it so you can explore what-if? Do you want a first-class refine/supersede relation, like 'h1 is actually …' in your cynder.lean?
- When you ask in Instruct mode, may the AI propose deletions, merges, or kind changes other than goal→derivation?
- Do you want a visible history of how your model evolved (your sketches keep successive versions)? May telelean run `git init` in model folders and auto-commit accepted changes?
- Arrows that point into an ASSUMPTION or start from a GOAL: should they be allowed, and what should they mean? Was 'from one section to another' meant to exclude arrows within a section?
- On the cards, do you want the full sentence always, or a short label (like your h1/h2) with the sentence on hover?
- Will you write nodes in languages other than English? This affects how ids are generated and which language the back-translation uses.
- S40 says 'click on the node and expand it'. Is it acceptable that click selects and a chevron expands instead?
- Should telelean copy your sentence into a Lean docstring by default (your S40 suggestion), and should DECISION records be a fifth node kind in your Lean files?
