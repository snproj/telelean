    - **severity:** nit
    - **where:** packages/webview/src/state/session.ts confirmExitSession, `track` and `result`
    - **problem:** Exit is allowed while a decision is in flight and overwrites `sessionBusy` with its own request. If Exit's answer arrives first, for example a refusal, `sessionBusy` becomes null while the decision is still pending.
