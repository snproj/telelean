# Slice 3 plan: FROZEN (spec v4.1)

*Integrator's plan, 2026-11-19, written on autopilot (the user is away and asked for the slices to be built without stopping; `verbatim_prompt_log/bundle_21/user_requested_documents/02_autopilot_decisions.md` logs every choice made without them). The specification is `verbatim_prompt_log/bundle_21/user_requested_documents/01_spec_v4.md`; clause IDs refer to it. The user's own words on freezing (`bundle_20/prompt20.md` lines 26–59: FROZEN 1–5, REVIEW 3, 5, 7, 8, INITIATING 2; `bundle_21/prompt21_midturn.md` message 1, ★D3) outrank every simplification here. Two designs fed this plan: the freeze engine and checks (core, runner), and the freeze UI, protocol and Undo (webview, host, core protocol); §7.1 resolves their conflicts. Every choice the spec leaves open, and every narrowing of it, is in §7.2 as an "AUTOPILOT DECISION" with its alternative and how to reverse it, cited as (D3-n). Slice 2's plan (`docs/slice2-plan.md`) is final: its §2 and §2.9 contracts are the interface this plan builds on, and its decisions are cited as (D2-n).*

*Designed for the simplest sound mechanism from the start (AP-24's lesson). There is one table of frozen targets with their expected values, and every state telelean judges is compared with it, whole-state. A state that breaks it is refused, never repaired. Slice 2's `judgeState`, reducer, session stack, write queue, base mask and end confirmation are reused, never paralleled.*

**Slice 3 = ★D8 (a) item 3, FROZEN:**
- **Freeze, Unfreeze, Freeze all other cards, Unfreeze all other cards** on cards (vocabulary cards included), single fields, arrows, "follows from nothing" marks and review items: on the Current view outside a session, and everywhere in a session, at any time, including while Claude works (FZ-1, FZ-2, FZ-6, FZ-9, FZ-10, FZ-13). Small narrowings, while Claude works, while the results are hidden, while telelean re-checks or applies, on Claude's suggested premises, for a freeze the running step can no longer meet, for "your version" over several items, and for a selection that can be frozen only in part, are logged (D3-6–D3-8, D3-30, D3-45, D3-46, D3-50, D3-53).
- **The mechanical check** (FZ-3, FZ-4): every judged state, and Apply's backstop, compare every frozen and held target with its frozen value: text directly, meaning in Lean through a new facts field `meaningKey` (D3-2), computed by a probe whose own code is never elaborated beside the model, so nothing in the model can change what it reports (D3-52). A step never moves frozen content to anything but its frozen value (§3.4's two-value rule, D3-5). A freeze made while a step runs that the step can no longer meet ends that step at once, not counted (D3-50). No input the runner may forget turns a check off (D3-51).
- **Reasons** (FZ-7): standing, accepted (RV-4: Accept now freezes) and yours. Starting a session with Formalize makes no binding reason (D3-1); editing a card's sentence, Why, stance or source on the board outside a session freezes that field, as a standing freeze (FROZEN 1, D3-44).
- **Decisions that would change frozen content** ask first, or are refused, by one check over the items a write takes out or brings back (FZ-10, D3-48); Submit's take-out leaves frozen content where it is (D3-49). What such a write changes only through elaboration order (an instance or coercion it adds, removes or moves) is not asked about; it shows as a stage problem at the next judge (D3-48's residual).
- **Unfreeze requests** (FZ-5): `request_unfreeze` pauses the step like a question, with Select them, Continue and Keep frozen. "Allow this change" is not built: held content changes only after the person unfreezes what holds it (D3-9).
- **The end** (FZ-8, EN-2, EN-3): standing freezes come back as they were, except what the person ticks **keep**; a freeze made on another subject's board comes back whatever is ticked, since no session writes another board's file (D3-41).
- **The look** (FZ-11), and "statements frozen" renamed "statements fixed" (FZ-12).
- **Undo** (UN-1, UN-2, UN-4, UN-5): standing freezes on the board stack, session freezes on the session stack.
- **What slice 2 deferred here:** Formalize's ▾ with **Open the whole model** and **Claude may add cards** (ES-2, D2-12); Change items of card fields, Delete, Remove arrow, Add of an intermediate card, Proof broken; the backstop's frozen and held rules (slice 2 §4.10's hook); the base mask, revisited (§3.10). They are built after FROZEN itself is complete and verified (§1): first the whole model open, then Claude may add cards. If that last part still has a blocker or major finding after its fix rounds, it moves to slice 4 with TR-7 (D3-43). A session's steps change only its own subject, and never what another subject's declarations mean, in every step; and a freeze of this subject's vocabulary made on another subject's board binds the session too, until the person unfreezes it in the session, which lifts it for that session only (D3-41).

**Not in slice 3:**
- **The transform window (slice 4):** the reasons `session-start` and `transform` exist in the types and are never made; TR-15's revaluing and the "Transform cards…" banner are hooks (§3.12).
- **References (slice 5):** `heldFields()` returns `[]` (FZ-4's `#` holding, RF-5).
- **Not built, by decision:** "Allow this change" and the "meaning changed by X" items, so RV-7's b5 stays unused (D3-9); "make it pending" on Proof broken (TR-14, D3-10); re-proving a Proof broken card in the session that broke it (D3-47).
- **Consistency checking** (M3).

### 2.1 Facts and the freeze snapshot (the fields in 3a; the probe in 3p)

```ts
// core facts.ts: DeclFacts +
/** D3-2: sha256 of the `Repr` of the elaborated declaration: its type; a definition's value (`defnInfo` only); an
 *  inductive's constructors with their types; its level parameters; and the same, in walk order, for every
 *  model-library constant the walk reaches that is not probed itself (auxiliaries such as `match_1` count for their
 *  parent). The walk follows types, definitions' values and constructors, never a theorem's value, so a nested proof
 *  (`_proof_n`) counts by its statement only. Before hashing, each such auxiliary is renamed by its place in the walk,
 *  macro scopes erased, so a raw generated name (`…._hyg.N`, which an unrelated declaration above it can change) never
 *  counts (S3-6). Every Expr it prints (the type, a definition's value, each constructor's type, each walked
 *  auxiliary's) is first normalised: every binder name of a `lam`, `forallE` or `letE` becomes `Name.anonymous`, and
 *  every `mdata` node is dropped, keeping what it wraps (ultra review round 2, S3-6; below). Absent when the probe gave
 *  none (`meaningMissing`). */
meaningKey?: string;
/** Why the probe gave no key, in facts with the `meaning` marker: the elaborated form is over 2 MB; the name cannot be
 *  passed to Lean (whitespace, guillemets); the probe's output was cut (runLean's 32 MB cap); the probe failed as a whole. */
meaningMissing?: MeaningMissing;
// core facts.ts, top level (ultra review round 2, L1: §2.2's stored values reuse it)
export type MeaningMissing = 'too-large' | 'unwritable' | 'cut' | 'probe-failed';
// core facts.ts: LeanFacts +
/** 3p's meaning probe (D3-52): present whenever the probe ran on these facts, whatever it gave (the facts' marker). */
meaning?: { status: 'ok' | 'cut' | 'failed' | 'oom' | 'timeout' | 'cancelled' | 'could-not-start'; durationMs: number;
  peakMemoryBytes?: number; detail?: string };

// core gate/types.ts: FrozenSnapshot +
declarations: Record<string, { kind?: string; type?: string; value?: string; meaningKey?: string }>;
/** Whole-model steps (§3.10): each card block's field lines at the freeze, `premises:` and `follows_from:` left out. */
blocks?: Record<string, string>;
```

- **The probe is isolated** (3p, D3-52; ultra review round 1, S3-1). Its own code is never elaborated in an environment that holds the model: the probe file imports only Lean's `Lean.CoreM` and `Lean.Util.FoldConsts`, and loads the model's built modules (the check file's imports) with `Lean.importModules` when it runs. So no instance, coercion, unexpander or notation of the model (a high-priority `ToString String` or `ForIn m Lean.Loop Unit` instance, say) can change how it walks, prints or hashes; and since the model is never imported beside Lean's modules, a model name that those modules also declare can no longer make its imports fail. refresh runs it as a second job under runLean, right after the check file (one heavy job at a time). For each probed declaration it prints its constant lists (`typeConsts`, `valueConsts`, by 1d's and 2b3's rules, unchanged) and one line `{"telelean-meaning-of":"<name>","text":"…"}`; the runner hashes the text and drops it; a text over 2 MB gives no key (`too-large`). The check file keeps only its `#check`, `#print`, `#print axioms` and `type_of%` probes. Old recordings (one job with the in-file value probe) still parse: their constants as before, with no meaningKey and no marker.
- **Binder names and `mdata` are not meaning** (ultra review round 2, S3-6). Many binder names are hygienic and take the module's macro-scope counter, which every command above them advances: an anonymous instance binder (`inst✝`), a `_` binder and a match's discriminant (`x✝`, `mkFreshBinderName`), a deriving handler's binders. The derived `Repr Expr` prints them, so without the normalisation a command inserted above a declaration (Claude's statement below its card's block, a proof lemma, a premise card written right before its target, a deriving clause) would change its key with nothing about its meaning changed. On the pinned 4.35 a plain arrow's binder takes a reserved scope (`elabArrow`) and is stable anyway; the tests keep one as a guard against a toolchain change. De Bruijn indices carry the binding, so no meaning is lost; a renamed binder is still a change of text, which every value that keeps text compares (§3.2), and where only meaning is kept (a held theorem's type) it is no change. `mdata` never changes what the kernel checks.
- **The ghost check keeps its in-file probe** (slice 1e's "Claude suggests" scratch check, `gate/scratch.ts`): its theorem is in no built module, so the isolated probe cannot load it. Its constants decide only the suggestion's B.2 pre-check, never a frozen comparison, and a written premise is judged by the isolated probe like any declaration (D3-52's residual).
- **`statement-changed`** also compares meaningKey: after a step's freeze, no declaration's meaning can change silently (§3.10). This closes slice 2's §4.3 residual for fixed statements. The freeze snapshot records it (`snapshotOf` copies `meaningKey`, 3a).
- **A missing key never passes** (D2-25's pattern; ultra review round 1, S3-2). Every comparison by meaningKey (frozen and held values, `statement-changed`, `existing-changed`, `other-subject`) in which the expected side has a key and the judged state's side has none refuses, at every purpose, with the token `no-key` under the comparison's own code, naming the declaration and the cause from `meaningMissing`: "telelean could not read what ‹name› means in Lean in this state: ‹cause›.", ‹cause› one of "its elaborated form is over 2 MB" · "its name cannot be passed to Lean: rename it" · "the meaning check's output was too large" · "the meaning check failed: ‹detail›". At step purposes it counts like any finding, except that a probe that failed as a whole is an `environment` outcome, as slice 1e's source-only facts are; at `stage` it is a `facts` problem; at `final` and in the backstop, `facts`. Only where the expected side itself has no key (a declaration the base gives none, or a value stored without one) does a comparison fall back to `approx` (D2-41's printed type and value plus the sorted constant sets), the name recorded as inexact; the final check and Apply refuse an inexact comparison (rule `frozen`) with "telelean could not compare what ‹name› means in Lean: ‹why›.", ‹why› the base's cause ("in your files its elaborated form is over 2 MB" · "in your files its name cannot be passed to Lean" · "the meaning check failed on your files: reload the window to check them again") or, for a value stored without a key, the cause stored with it (`AddressValue.missing`, `HeldValue.missing`, §2.2) when that cause is permanent, since freezing again would store the same keyless value (ultra review round 2, L1): "its name cannot be passed to Lean: rename it" (`unwritable`) · "its elaborated form is over 2 MB" (`too-large`); otherwise (a cut output, a probe that failed, facts without the `meaning` marker) "it was frozen before telelean could read its meaning: unfreeze it and freeze it again" ("put ‹item› back to review and accept it again" for an accepted item).
- **Base facts** (S3-2, FID-4, L1 of the ultra review): base-facts.json gets the `meaning` marker whenever 3p's probe ran on it. When the marker is absent (facts written before 3p), or the probe failed on the base as a whole, the first judge after a window load runs `checkBase` once more on the base commit (which never changes) and replaces the file: at most once per load, never because some names lack keys. `recover()`'s upgrade of an older session does it at once (§5.2).
- **No probe-off mode** (D3-2). With the probe isolated, AP-21's second run without it is gone, and Apply needs every key it compares (D2-25); nothing is applied on printed comparisons alone.

## 3. The freeze engine and the checks (core; runner where named)

### 3.1 Invariants
- **One table.** At every judged state, `effectiveFreezes` turns the reasons into one table of frozen targets with their expected values, and `frozenFindings` compares the state with it and with the held maps. Nothing is incremental; nothing is repaired.
- **Out of Claude's reach** (FZ-3, S3). freezes.json sits in `.telelean/`, which the shadow never copies into ws/ and which the path guard (`HIDDEN_SEGMENTS`) and G0 refuse; session freezes sit in run.json in VS Code's storage. Claude learns of freezes only from turns and tool results, and every answer about them is computed from the store.
- **Only the person unfreezes** (FZ-6; FROZEN 2, 3). A reason goes only by the person's Unfreeze (or its Undo), the person's own status change of the covering item (a cascade they confirmed included), a dialog choice the person made (§3.7), or the session's end (FZ-8). Reconcile, judges, Claude's steps and requests never remove one (tested as a property).
- **Frozen binds Claude only** (FZ-1). The person's own edits outside a session keep their freezes (a record names a target, not a value), and an edit of a card's sentence, Why, stance or source freezes that field, as a standing freeze in the same board step (FROZEN 1, D3-44); the person's arrow into a card replaces its mark, even a frozen one (A22); deleting a card through telelean drops its records (A6, §2.6).
- **One subject** (D3-41; R2-13 of review round 2), in every step, narrow or whole-model. G0's `outside-subject` file rule (M2, kept by slice 2 at every purpose of a session) refuses any change to another subject's files, new declarations included, so their text never changes. The `other-subject` rule (§3.5) refuses, at every judged state, a change of the session's subject that changes what another subject's declarations mean: their meaningKeys, and the held values of the session's subject's declarations that they reach in B. That keeps every freeze another board made on its own subject's content. A freeze another board made on this subject's vocabulary (TR-10 lets S1's board freeze S2's `InGame`, which S1 uses) is on content the session may change, so the session's table holds it too: the start snapshot reads it from that board's freezes.json as a foreign record, expected at B; the person may unfreeze it in the session, which lifts it for that session only, since no session writes another board's file (§2.2, §3.3, §3.6; ultra review round 1 and round 2, FID-1). So no standing freeze of any board can be broken but by the person's own Unfreeze.
- **The base never moves** (ES-7). Nothing writes freezes.json during a session; kept freezes reach it only at Apply (inside its change set) or after Exit (one board step) (§5.4).

### 3.2 Values (`addressValue`, `heldValue`; 3a)
Each target's value, as canonical JSON hashed with `review/hash.ts`:
- **sentence, kind, why, stance, source:** the block's field lines as G5 reads them; absent is empty.
- **statement:** the binding mode, the bound statement's name, and each bound declaration (the statement abbrev with its docstring mirror; an AXIOM's `.holds`) as `{ name, text (trailing spaces trimmed), meaningKey }`. No binding is "empty": that is keep-empty (FZ-2).
- **dependencies:** the sorted `premises:` entries and `follows_from`.
- **proof:** empty, pending or proved, and the `.holds` text; its `decls` are `[T.holds]`, so its held closure is its type's (FZ-4).
- **vocabulary declaration:** its text without the docstring, its kind and meaningKey (constructors and fields are in meaningKey). **gloss:** the docstring. **arrow, mark:** present or absent.
- `approx` replaces meaningKey with D2-41's fields; `exact` holds when every covered declaration has a meaningKey; `missing` keeps, for each one without, its `meaningMissing` from the facts the value was computed on (ultra review round 2, L1).
- **`heldValue`** (FZ-4, Appendix B "Held sets", D3-3): a theorem, axiom, opaque or Prop-valued declaration by its type's meaning only; anything else by its text without the docstring (whitespace collapsed), its kind and meaningKey; `absent` when missing.

### 3.3 The table (`effectiveFreezes`; 3a)
- **Standing:** the session's snapshot minus `standingCleared` (outside sessions, the file's records), plus the session's `foreign` records (D3-41), likewise minus `standingCleared` (the person's Unfreeze in the session, ultra review round 2, FID-1); expected: B's value.
- **Accepted:** each record with `frozen` gives `acceptCovers` of its draft, always, read from `items`, whatever `freezes` holds (D3-51). The targets the item's content owns are checked through the item (§3.4 step 1); the others (a Formalize item's sentence) keep B's value.
- **Yours:** each OwnFreeze, with its value.
- Several reasons on one target make one entry whose `expect` lists every expectation. They agree by construction: once frozen, only the person changes a value, and the person edits only outside sessions, where the value is the file's. If they ever differ, the state must equal each (fail closed).
- **Not found** (A6): targets whose card, declaration or arrow is in neither B nor the state are listed in `notFound`; their expectation stays B's (absent).

### 3.4 The check (`frozenFindings`; 3a)
1. **Accepted items** (F4 of review round 1: checked exactly like an own freeze). Each record with `frozen`: its draft by key exists with `contentKey` equal to its frozen key (a Delete's or Remove arrow's draft too, so re-adding is refused, RV-4), else `frozen` (`accepted`); each name of `frozen.held` is checked in step 3 like an own freeze's. The frozen key is `frozen.fingerprint`, except for an accepted Add premise, whose key is `accepted.fingerprint`: slice 2's premise write moves it, in its step 4, from the draft's key to the written item's key, which differs (§3.8 of slice 2), and an un-written or taken-out premise has the draft's key in both (ultra review round 1, IMP-2). In the judgement of its own write (`writePremise`; recognised by a ledger `premise` entry for the item while its record's `fingerprint` is still `premiseDraftKey` of its draft), the premise being written is checked by that write's render (slice 2 §3.8), not here, so its write never fails on its own key.
2. **Every other entry.** `addressValue(current)` against each expected value: by exact keys when both are exact; when the expected value is exact and the state's is not, `no-key` for each declaration whose key the state lacks (§2.1); when the expected value itself is inexact, by `approx`, the names recorded in `inexact` with their ‹why›. The same three cases hold for each held name in step 3, and in the `other-subject`, `existing-changed` and `statement-changed` comparisons. Tokens: `field`; `keep-empty` (expected empty); `meaning` (texts equal, meaning differs); `arrow`; `dependencies`; `mark`; `deleted` (the target's card is gone); `declaration`; `gloss`.
3. **Held** (FZ-4). Each name's expected held value comes from:
   - roots expected from B: `heldMap(B, decls)`, memoised (B is fixed);
   - own freezes and accepted items: their stored `held` maps.

   Each name is compared with `heldValue(current, name)`, token `changed`: every held expectation of every holder, the roots of standing and own entries included (ultra review round 1, S3-3), and an accepted item's `frozen.held` names with its own, since step 1's content key has no meaningKey (R2-9 of review round 2). Two different held expectations for one name give `held` (`conflict`), fail closed. A name whose own entry (step 2) already has a finding at this state drops its `held` (`changed`) finding (one message), never a `conflict`. So a root's own freeze never hides another holder's different expectation of it: no state meets both, and a finding stays until the person unfreezes one of them. Each `held` finding names its holders, which also feed the views' chain marks. FZ-4's exceptions hold by construction: a target's Proof (a `.holds` holds by its type), an empty statement (no declaration) and Dependencies additions (not Lean) are in no held closure. Slice 5 hook: `heldFields(model, entries)` returns `[]`.
4. **The two-value rule** (step purposes only; F2 of review round 1, D3-5). At step purposes, a frozen target, a held name or an accepted item gives a finding only when its value in the judged state differs both from its expected value and from its value at the step's S₀: the same functions applied to `stepStart` (`addressValue`, `heldValue`, and the content key of the item's draft among `stepStart.drafts`). So a step may leave a difference it found at its start, and may put back the frozen value, but can never move frozen content to anything else. A difference found at S₀ (an earlier step of the same queue changed what the person froze while it ran, a stage problem not resolved yet, a judge's revert) stays a stage problem: at `stage` and `final`, and in the backstop, only the expected value counts.
5. **Fresh** (FZ-10). A finding is `fresh` when the `madeAt` of its target (and of each holder, for `held`) is later than `since`. `madeAt` is set by every freeze action, Undo and Redo that gives a key a standing or own reason (§2.2), so a freeze restored by an Undo during a step is fresh too (F3 of review round 1).

Messages for Claude (`CLAUDE_FREEZE_TEXTS`):
- frozen: "“‹sentence›” (‹id›): its ‹field› is frozen (‹reasons›). Leave it exactly as it was: ‹show›. To change it, ask the person first with request_unfreeze."
- keep-empty: "“‹sentence›” (‹id›): its ‹field› is frozen empty: leave it empty (remove what you wrote)."
- meaning: "‹name› is frozen: its text is unchanged, but what it means in Lean changed (something placed before it, or something it uses, elaborates differently): restore what it meant."
- arrow, dependencies, mark: "The arrow “‹s›” → “‹t›” is frozen: put it back." · "“‹t›”'s Dependencies are frozen: add no arrow into it and remove none." · "“‹t›” is frozen as following from nothing: leave its mark."
- deleted: "“‹c›” has frozen fields, so it cannot be deleted: restore it."
- accepted: "The person accepted “‹label›”: it is frozen as accepted; restore it exactly."
- held: "‹name› is held by frozen ‹cards›: frozen Lean relies on it, so its text and meaning may not change. Restore it, or ask with request_unfreeze (the person can unfreeze what holds it)."

### 3.5 Where it runs (3a)
- **`judgeState`, every purpose** (`JudgeInput.frozenInput`; the table is built after computeItems):
  - step purposes and `final`: findings join `global` (refusals), at step purposes under the two-value rule (§3.4 step 4, with computeItems on `stepStart` memoised per S₀ model, §2.9; for `engine.complete`'s gate, which runs outside any step, S₀ is the `adopt` commit it starts from); a `no-key` finding refuses at every purpose (§2.1); at `final`, an `inexact` comparison refuses (rule `frozen`, §2.1), and so does a call without the session's freezes (D3-51);
  - `stage`: findings become `problems` with code `frozen` and the person's text: §3.8's for a change an earlier step of the queue made, and otherwise "“‹label›” is frozen, but the working copy differs from its frozen version (‹item› changed it): unfreeze it, or decline ‹item›." (without the item when none owns the difference). Apply is disabled until they go, Submit is not, and a later queue's steps keep them as they are (§3.4 step 4);
  - `existing-changed` drops its finding for a name that has a `frozen` or `held` finding (one message);
  - `freezeAtAccept` is filled for every draft of the judgement, whatever its record's status and whether it has a record yet: the held map of the item's own declarations' meaning closure, own names included; for an unwritten Add premise, the held values of the names its statement reaches and of their closure (§2.3). Reconcile, which runs after judgeState and creates the records of new drafts, copies the entry into `heldAtJudge` of every record it leaves in review, new and returned ones included (D3-51; ultra review round 1, IMP-1). So the records a queue-end judge creates can be accepted at once, and an item returned to review never keeps a held map from an older version.
- **Other subjects** (D3-41; R2-13 of review round 2), at every purpose and in every step, narrow ones too, beside the frozen findings and like them (under the two-value rule at step purposes, problems at `stage`, refusals at `final` and in the backstop), token `other-subject` (`existing-changed`):
  - every declaration of another subject keeps its meaningKey (§3.4 step 2's three cases: exact, `no-key`, or D2-41's comparison recorded as inexact when B gives no key). G0's `outside-subject` already keeps its text; this catches what a declaration of the session's subject (an instance, a coercion) changes in how it elaborates;
  - every declaration of the session's subject that another subject's declarations reach in B keeps its held value (`heldMap` over those declarations, restricted to the session's subject, memoised: B is fixed).

  The same comparison makes slice 2's step limits exact (FZ-3: a step limit's value is compared in Lean): `existing-changed` compares meaningKey for every declaration of S₀, with the same three cases.

### 3.6 Freeze actions (`planFreeze`, the reducer's `freeze`; 3b, written by 3d)
- **Expansion:** a card → its eight fields (D3-20); its Dependencies cover the arrows into it and its mark, not those out of it (FZ-1). A vocabulary card → declaration and gloss. An item → `acceptCovers` at Claude's proposal (D3-30); hidden Results and their parts are not listed, so never selected. Derived data (CF-7: statuses, bands, Used by, warning arrows) is never a subject.
- **Freeze** adds the view's reason: on the board, `standing`; in a session, `yours`, with the value of the judged state the view shows, unmasked, and its held map, which the runner computes: J during a queue, the stage judgement's commit in a stage, F in confirming. J is the stage judgement the queue started from; during the session's first queue, when there is none yet (stage 0), J is the base commit with the base facts (F12 of review round 1). A value equal to B's whose held map also equals `heldMap(B, decls)` is stored as `{ from: 'base' }`; otherwise as `{ from: 'state', commit, value, held }` (R2-1 of review round 2). So a card whose own text and meaningKey are B's, but which uses a definition a step changed, is frozen with that definition's new version, as the person saw it, and accepting or keeping that change raises no `held` problem. `{ from: 'base' }` is otherwise "your version" (§3.7, FZ-10): B's text and B's meaning.
- **Unfreeze** removes every reason (FZ-7): a standing one leaves the file (board) or goes into `standingCleared` (session; a foreign one too, D3-41, whose board's file keeps it, so it is in force again when the session ends); own freezes go; an accepted item covering the target returns to review with RV-5's list (`needs-confirmation` first), only in a stage (D3-7, D3-35); an item in review or to be redone whose `frozen` covers it (D3-4) loses its `frozen`, likewise only in a stage.
- **All other cards** (FZ-9): the containing cards are the selected cards, the cards of selected fields and items, both ends of each selected arrow, marked cards and selected vocabulary cards; the others are every axiom, proposition and vocabulary card of the subject outside them, each as a whole card. Unfreeze all other cards removes standing reasons (in a session foreign ones too, for the session, counted with the standing ones; on a board never a foreign one, D3-41) and own reasons, and accepted ones only with "also un-accept" (`unaccept`, stage only). It always asks first, with the counts (D3-25).
- **Versions** (FZ-10). Freezing a target that an item in review owns offers Claude's proposal (the item stays in review) or your version (the item is rejected, then the target frozen at B's value: one `decideItems` with `freezeSubjects`). By kind: Add, Add arrow, Proof broken, new vocabulary: proposal only; Formalize, Change, changed vocabulary, a shown Result: both; Delete, Remove arrow: yours only ("Freeze and reject the deletion"). When one freeze reaches targets of several items in review, one confirmation lists them all, with every other list the action needs (Filling now, the whole-model question, RV-5's, the counts; §2.4), and resending with `confirmed` naming every list it showed freezes Claude's proposal for every listed target; "your version" is offered only when exactly one item is involved, and goes through `decideItems` with `freezeSubjects` (R2-11 of review round 2, D3-30). A Delete or Remove arrow among several items is then left (`left`: "Freeze it on its own: freezing it rejects the deletion.").
- **Not freezable** (`left`, with the reason; D3-6, revised in the ultra review's round 1, FID-7 and U5): while the results are hidden, the Proof, Dependencies and mark of every target that has a Result in this session: "“‹T›”'s proof, Dependencies and mark can be frozen once its result is shown."; while its step runs, the running target's: "Claude's step for “‹T›” is running, and what it has proposed for it cannot be withdrawn: freeze its proof, Dependencies or mark once its result is shown." A target of the running queue whose step has not started yet, with no Result in the session, can have them frozen: its step reads the table when it starts, as for any freeze made before a step. Also not freezable: a provisional card; a "Claude suggests" premise draft that is not accepted ("Accept it first: a suggested premise is not in the working copy.", D3-45); derived data. Not unfreezable on a board, outside a session: a foreign record (D3-41): "Frozen on ‹S›'s board: unfreeze it there." (that board is unlocked then). In a session, where every other board is read-only, its Unfreeze lifts it for the session only (ultra review round 2, FID-1).
- **Phases** (D3-7, D3-8, D3-46). In every phase, while the session is `judging` (a judge runs or is due, or the write queue holds a write), the four actions and the Undo and Redo of a freeze action are refused at once, "Re-checking the working copy: freeze again in a moment.", and the webview disables them with that reason; nothing waits (D2-74, F17 of review round 1).

- **Confirming** (ultra review round 1, L6, L7). After each freeze action, and each Undo or Redo of one, in confirming (§4.9), the runner runs the frozen part of the final check again, pure and with no Lean job (`frozenFindings` on F with the new end table, the `other-subject` rule and the inexact rule), and updates `confirmation`, so a failed check that the person's Unfreeze resolves no longer reads failed. - **Writes** (3d). In a stage or confirming, a freeze action is a records-only write through the session's write queue, like Accept; an un-accepted written premise is taken out in it (slice 2 §3.8). During a step, ws/ and the item records are untouched (D2-62, D2-63): the runner writes a copy of the session with the reducer's outcome applied (the new `freezes`, and the stack entry and `nextAction` the action pushed) through `writeStateChecked`, as one task of the run's chain of run.json writes, and only when that write succeeds replaces the in-memory session's `freezes`, `stack` and `nextAction` (every field the outcome changed) with the written copy's, before the chain's next task runs (ultra review round 1, L2). A failed write refuses the action ("telelean could not save the session: ‹error›") and changes nothing in memory, so neither the step's next write nor `dispose()` can persist it (AF11; F11 of review round 1). The step's own later writes carry the new `freezes`, stack and `nextAction`, since they write the in-memory session.

### 3.7 Accept and the item's freeze (RV-4, FZ-7; 3b)
- **Accept** (own or group) sets `ItemRecord.frozen` to `{ fingerprint: the record's fingerprint, held: the record's heldAtJudge, at }`, and is refused (`blocked` in a group) when the record has no `heldAtJudge` (D3-51), so its covered targets are frozen: Add: the new card's fields; Add premise: those and its arrow; Add arrow: the arrow; Formalize, Change: sentence and statement plus the changed fields; Result: the proof; Proof broken: the proof, kept empty; Vocabulary: each term, a presupposition with its helper instance; Delete, Remove arrow: nothing, but the item's own check refuses re-adding.
- **It ends** with the person's own status change of the item (a decline, back to review), an Unfreeze that covers it (§3.6), or a cascade of the person's action that changes the item's status, whatever that status. RV-5's confirmation lists every such item, accepted or not, as "frozen: you accepted it" (D2-64's rule while the results are hidden), and confirming clears its `frozen` (R2-4 of review round 2). Reconcile's returns to review (D2-23, RV-11, the defensive RV-5 rule) keep it: the row reads "in review · frozen (you accepted it)" (D3-4). So when the person then rejects a returned definition, its returned dependents are in that one confirmation too, and none is left as a `frozen (accepted)` stage problem. Undo restores it with the rest of ItemState. A written premise's step 4 also sets `frozen.held` to the written item's `freezeAtAccept` entry from that write's judgement, which now covers the premise's own declarations and their meaning (3d, `run/premises.ts`); its content key needs no rewrite, since §3.4 step 1 reads an accepted Add premise's key from `accepted.fingerprint`, which slice 2's step 4 already moves to the written key (ultra review round 1, IMP-2). Until 3d, a written premise keeps the held map of its Accept (what its statement reaches).
- **Decisions that would change frozen content** (FZ-10, FZ-1; F6 of review round 1, R2-17 of review round 2, D3-48). One rule, from items, replaces a rule for each path. Ownership is total and disjoint (computeItems), and every take-out restores B's version of exactly its items' content (`planItemRevert`), so what a decision's write changes of frozen content follows from the items it takes out and brings back, by meaning, with no planned files and no Lean job. Before the runner enqueues a decision's write, it runs `frozenChangesOf` on the stage judgement's drafts, with the table as the action would leave it:
  - an item the write **takes out** (a decline and its cascade, DO-0's included; a premise take-out; the Undo of an un-reject, or of a written premise's Accept) lists each frozen target and held name it owns whose expected value differs from B's. An accepted item's own content goes only with the item itself, which RV-5's confirmation asks about (it lists every `frozen` item the cascade changes, above), so its targets never appear here; only its `frozen.held` names count, and only for an item outside the cascade (an `items` holder, which itemDeps' walk normally puts in the cascade);
  - an item the write **brings back** (un-reject, the Undo of a decline) lists each frozen target and held name it owns, but the keys whose reasons the same Undo restores from the action's recorded `freezes` (`restored`).

  An item owns: a `card:<c>` item c's fields (a Delete also c's arrows out and its mark); a `proof:<c>` item c's Proof; an arrow, Remove arrow or premise item its arrow and its target's Dependencies (a premise item also its target's mark, its new card's fields, and, while its completion is written, its target's Proof); every item the vocabulary entries and held names among its `provides.decls`. Then:
  - **a decline** (Reject, plain, DO-0, with their cascades) whose list is not empty answers `needs-confirmation` with `freeze.frozenChanges`, in the same dialog as RV-5's list when there is one: "These are frozen and would change: ‹list›." [Unfreeze them and decline] [Keep them frozen at your version] [Cancel]. The person's choice comes back as the message's `freeze`. `unfreeze` removes, in the same action, every standing and own reason of the listed targets and of the listed held names' holders, and clears `frozen` on a listed item holder (an accepted one returns to review, FZ-7). `yours` revalues those own freezes as `{ from: 'base' }`, which is what the decline restores; it is offered when every declined decision is a Reject or plain and no row is held by an item, and if a listed freeze's held map still differs from B's afterwards (it rests on another change that stays), the next judge shows that as a stage problem, fail closed. The decide step records `freezes: { before, after }`, so Undo restores both. This is FZ-10's "Rejecting an item whose proposal you froze first asks whether to unfreeze it", and it also covers a cascade that reaches content the person froze, through any reason but an accepted one;
  - **a Shaky decline** changes no file and no freeze: what the person froze of the item stays frozen, and Claude reworks the rest at Submit, where the take-out leaves frozen content in place (§3.10, D3-49). So "decline it Shaky, but keep this field" works whichever the person does first; round 1's "[Shaky and unfreeze it]" dialog is gone;
  - **un-reject, back to review of a written premise, and the Undo or Redo of a decision**, whose list is not empty are refused: "‹list› is frozen (or held by frozen ‹cards›) and would change: unfreeze it first, or decide it by hand." Refusing is the simpler sound choice here: the person can unfreeze, then repeat. A Redo of a decline re-sends its recorded `freeze` choice (§2.3), so the check runs again and that choice applies to what it lists then; a Redo without one is refused when the list is not empty;
  - **the judge's reverts** are not asked about: they become stage problems (§3.5).

  The check reads the stage judgement's drafts and the table at the moment of the decision. If a write queued ahead of it moves the head (D2-27 lets decisions queue while judging), whatever more the write then changes is caught by the next judge's frozen check as a stage problem, fail closed.

  **Residual** (D3-48, D3-49; ultra review round 1, S3-4). Ownership covers what an item's content is, not what it changes in how other declarations elaborate. A take-out or bring-back that adds, removes or moves an instance, a coercion or an attributed declaration can change what a frozen declaration means although that instance is in no frozen closure (and D3-39 keeps position out of every value): for example, rejecting the removal of an instance `instOld` that a frozen card's statement elaborated without after the step. Such a change is not asked about first; the next judge shows it as a stage problem (§3.5's text, without an item when none owns the difference), Apply waits, and the Undo of the decision takes it back, fail closed, as for the judge's own reverts.
- **An Add premise's Accept** (RV-1, AR-5, D3-36) is gated, with `Gate.frozen` (Select the frozen targets), when its target's Dependencies or mark is frozen: "“‹T›”'s Dependencies are frozen: unfreeze them to accept this premise."; and when its target's Proof is frozen while the target is not proved in the stage judgement, since the Accept's completion would write it (F6): "“‹T›”'s proof is frozen: accepting this premise completes it." A due premise write or completion fails only on a frozen or held finding its write introduces (ultra review round 1, S3-5): one that the stage judgement of the write's parent commit does not have, which is the two-value rule (§3.4 step 4) with S₀ = the write's parent commit, as `complete`'s gate already has with the `adopt` commit (§3.5); the premise being written is never one (§3.4 step 1). Such a write fails like a write that could not be made: nothing of it is kept, and the stage gets the problem "“‹item›” was accepted, but writing it would change frozen ‹x›: unfreeze it, or decline the item.", fail closed (a freeze cannot interleave, since freezing is refused while the write queue holds a write, §3.6). A frozen problem the stage already had (an earlier step changed content frozen meanwhile, §3.8) never fails an unrelated premise write.

### 3.8 Freezing while Claude works (FZ-10, D3-5; runner in 3d)
- **The version you see.** A freeze during a queue keeps J's value, the state the person sees (D2-63; the base during the session's first queue, §3.6); `sessionFreeze.view` must equal `viewVersion`.
- **Claude is told** in its next tool result (`personChangedFreezes`), and the next turn's FROZEN section has it.
- **Checks.** Every later check, freeze, gate and the queue's end judge it. A gate that fails only on fresh findings is not counted as an attempt (`toolFinish` skips the count when every blocking finding is fresh, D3-19). After the gate passes, the finish runs `frozenFindings` again with the store as it is then, so a freeze made during the gate's Lean job is never missed; a finding fails the finish like a failed gate, counted unless fresh. The queue's end judge cannot miss one either (§3.5).
- **Rollback** (F3 of review round 1). A step during which any freeze action, Undo or Redo gave a key a reason (some `madeAt` later than the step's start) rolls back to S₀ when it gives up, not to `lastCommitSince`: a passing commit made before the freeze may hold Claude's change to the target, while at S₀ every target has J's value or a difference the step found there (§3.4 step 4).
- **Times that survive a reload** (ultra review round 1, L5). The step's start is `PersistedStep.startedAt`, written in its `step-start` record, and `since` is persisted with the step's run.json writes (§2.3); the rollback, `unmeetable` and `fresh` read only these, never a time held in memory. On Resume, `since` becomes the time the resume turn is sent, since its FROZEN section tells Claude everything frozen then; a freeze made before the reload keeps its `madeAt`, so it still counts as made during the step. A slice-2 step interrupted at the upgrade gets its `startedAt` from the upgrade: its `step-start` record's time, or the upgrade's own when that record has none, since no `madeAt` exists before the upgrade and any earlier time then works (§5.2; ultra review round 2, L1).
- **A freeze the step cannot meet** (R2-7 of review round 2, D3-50). Some freezes made during a step cannot be met by any state the step may still reach: content its freeze_statements fixed (`statement-changed`, with `FrozenSnapshot.blocks` in whole-model steps, refuses changing it back), and content one of its own live ledger entries writes (every normalise re-applies an `unarrow`, `delete`, `proof-broken` or `arrow` entry, and Claude cannot withdraw one). At every check, freeze, gate and finish, core's `unmeetable` picks the frozen and held findings whose key, or a holder, got its reason during the step (`madeAt` later than the step's start) and that the step's freeze snapshot fixes, with `statement-changed` passing for them, or that one of the step's own live ledger entries writes. If there is one, the step ends at once as given up, not counted, rolled back to S₀ (above); its Result reads not proved, with "You froze ‹x› after Claude's step had already changed it." (shown as any outcome is, RV-6), and the queue goes on. Any other freeze made during a step costs Claude only the uncounted check (D3-19). `propose_arrow` and `mark_proof_broken` refuse at once the two entries that could only end this way (§2.8).
- **Earlier steps of the same queue.** The two-value rule (§3.4 step 4) lets a step leave a difference an earlier step of its queue made to what the person froze while the queue ran, so the step is not blamed for it; it may not change that content further. At the queue's end the stage gets the problem "“‹label›” is frozen at the version you saw when you froze it, but Claude's step for “‹T›” had already changed it: unfreeze it, or decline ‹item›.", with ‹item› and ‹T› taken from the item that owns the difference (its `origin`), and Apply waits until the person resolves it (a Reject restores B's version, which resolves it when B's is the frozen one). Later queues keep it as it is under the same rule, so their steps do not fail on it. D3-6 keeps Results' parts out of this, so the problem never names hidden content.
- **Filling now.** Freezing an empty field that the running queue may fill first asks (the runner's `needs-confirmation` with `fillingNow`, passed on by the host): "Claude may be filling “‹card›” › ‹field› now. Freeze it empty? Claude will be told that it must stay empty." Such a field is an empty Lean statement at a selected arrow of the queue; and, while any step of the queue has the whole model open, any empty Lean statement, Why, stance or source of a card of the subject (F8 of review round 1). The rule reads only the queue's StepScopes and J, never what Claude did. The dialog adds: "If Claude has already fixed its statements, its step ends without a result." (D3-50).

So while any step of the running queue has the whole model open, every action that gives content of the subject a reason (Freeze, Freeze all other cards, a freeze of a review item, the Undo of an Unfreeze, the Redo of a Freeze) first asks (`needs-confirmation` with `wholeModel`; through slice 2's `confirmed` for an Undo or Redo): "Claude's whole-model step may already have changed ‹x›. - **Unfreeze** during a step: Claude is told, and may then change the target within its step's limits. Accepted content waits for the step's end (D3-7).

### 3.9 Unfreeze requests (FZ-5; runner in 3d; D3-9, D3-24)

X's item then lists the statements that rely on it, as every definition change does ("Your statements rely on it", slice 2 §5.6). The cost, which the user guide states (F13 of review round 1): unfreezing a holder A also lets Claude rewrite A itself, and freezing A again holds X again, so "keep A's words but let X change" cannot be expressed; the person reviews A's rewrite as an item and can Reject it.

### 3.10 The whole model open, and new cards (ES-2's switches, D2-12; core in 3c1 and 3c2, runner in 3e2 and 3e3)
- **Switches.** `formalize.options` is stored on the request's inferences (`SessionInference.options`). - **Submit's take-out keeps frozen content** (R2-3 of review round 2, D3-49), in every Submit queue, narrow or whole-model. The frozen part's held names keep what it rests on fixed, so D2-57's reason for the take-out does not arise for that part; the kept item's unfrozen parts stay fixed for the other steps of the queue (the limits below), which must then keep to them.
- **Submit with whole-model steps** (D3-42; F7 of review round 1). - **Origins** (D2-43's rule, extended). - **Whole-model arrows** (D3-38; ultra review round 1, IMP-6).   - `mark_proof_broken`: a card that is not a target, whose `.holds` is an unchanged theorem of B with a Lean error in the last check, its proof not frozen and its `.holds` not held (D3-50); telelean takes out `proofMaterialOf(current, ‹c›.holds)`, so a lemma shared by two broken proofs goes with the second.

## 4. The UI (webview; 3f, 3g)

### 4.1 Principles
- **The host owns the freeze state.** The webview renders `graph.freezes` (standing, on the Current view) and `session.freezes` (on the Proposed view, the review panel, dialogs, the end confirmation, request cards), and previews rules with the same core functions the host enforces with (`planFreeze`, `frozenNeeds`), from the view's data as it is (§2.9). The preview decides enablement and the first dialog only; lists only the host computes (Filling now, the whole-model question, RV-5's cascade) come with its `needs-confirmation` (§2.4).
- **Not optimistic.** A lock appears or goes only when the host's graph or session shows it; the pressed control shows a spinner until `actionResult` (`freezeBusy`).
- **Never without a gesture.** No code path posts `setFreezes` or `sessionFreeze` on its own; FZ-8's restore is the host's.
- **Routing** (UN-1, ES-9; ultra review round 1, U2): no session → `setFreezes` (board stack). During a session every freeze action is a `sessionFreeze` (session stack), and what decides its subjects is where they were selected, never the focus (a click in the Context Menu section or a popover moves the focus anyway): review items, item-detail rows, request rows, end-confirmation rows and stage problems are session subjects on either view (`FieldRef.from: 'session'`); a board selection counts only on the Proposed view (`from: 'board'`), and on the Current view it is never a subject, its entries disabled with "During an edit session your files can't change: freeze on the Proposed view."; another subject's board during a session → slice 2's RunElsewhere text; phase applying → "Applying…". The Proposed view exists from the session's start and shows J (the base during the first queue); [Select them] on a request card, Select the frozen targets on `Gate.frozen` and [Select it] on a stage problem switch the board to it before selecting.
- **RV-6 by construction:** the host builds the freeze views on the view's model; no reason, row or label comes from a hidden Result or a Result's part.

## 5. Undo and persistence

### 5.1 freezes.json and the board stack (UN-2; 3b, 3d)
- A board freeze: `set-freezes` → `planAction` (a delta, §2.6) → `materialisePlan` merges the delta into freezes.json as it reads it then, and writes it → `UndoStacks` records the whole-file change it made (in memory, per subject, as every board step); `layoutOnly` (no snapshot) unless it wrote an id (D3-16, D3-21). An edit of a card's sentence, Why, stance or source carries its field's new standing record in its own plan, so the edit and its freeze are one board step (D3-44).
- Undo and Redo apply the whole-file change backwards, all or nothing; a conflict refuses with "freezes.json changed since: freeze or unfreeze by hand".
- Not a History sidecar (`isSubjectSidecarFile` unchanged): History restores never touch it (D3-15). ModelWorkspace reads it on load, on its sidecar event, and inside `reread` after every write it makes itself (perform, Undo, Redo, `applyChanges`, the end's board step; ultra review round 1, L10), so its `freezes(subject)` is never older than the file when a session starts, and a hand edit is read too; unreadable records are listed (A6), and the person's next board freeze change rewrites the file without them, its toast naming them.

### 5.2 The session's freezes in run.json (3d)
- `PersistedSession.freezes` is written with the session's first run.json write (the snapshot). `start` reads the snapshot from disk, after the board queue has drained and in the same step as the base commit (ultra review round 1, L10), so a board freeze made just before the start is in it; the foreign records (D3-41) are read in the same step.
- **The upgrade** (R2-15 of review round 2; ultra review round 1, L1, FID-10; ultra review round 2, L1). `recover()` gives a slice-2 session `freezes` from freezes.json as it is then (nothing wrote it during the session), with the foreign records, empty `own`, `standingCleared`, `requests` and `madeAt`, and `upgrade: 'pending'` (§2.2). It runs after slice 2's own recovery (the write in flight finished, Apply's recovery done), on the phase that recovery leaves; when base-facts.json lacks the `meaning` marker (§2.1), as a slice-2 session's does, it runs `checkBase` at once, in every phase. Then:
  - **In a stage** (a judge that was running included): a judge with a build, as slice 2's own v1 conversion does, and the session is `judging` until both end. Its judge-apply reconciles as usual, which sets `heldAtJudge` on every record in review, then sets `frozen` on every accepted record that has none (`{ fingerprint: its accepted fingerprint, held: that judgement's freezeAtAccept entry, at: now }`), and removes `upgrade`. So RV-4 holds for what the session accepted before the upgrade, in a later whole-model step too, every held map is exact, and every item in review can be accepted at once.
  - **During a queue** (a step interrupted, or a queue waiting between steps or right after its `queue-start` write): only `checkBase`. The records stay as they are (D2-62, D2-63): a judge-apply on the head would reconcile them against half a step's work. The interrupted step gets `startedAt` from its `step-start` record's time, or the upgrade's own when that record has none (no `madeAt` exists yet, so any earlier time works, §3.8), and Resume sets `since` as for any step. The queue-end judge (D2-63: also after Cancel step or a failure), which builds whenever `upgrade` is pending, so that its facts have keys, sets `frozen` in its judge-apply as above, and removes `upgrade`. Until then accepted content is protected only as slice 2 protected it (its step limits; `frozen` is not set yet), and no Accept is possible during a step anyway. A freeze the person makes meanwhile takes J's value from slice 2's judged facts, without keys, so the final check later asks to freeze it again (§2.1's transient text), fail closed.
  - **In confirming** (its check running again included): the final check runs again, which builds F's facts afresh; from its judgement the upgrade's own records-only write sets `frozen` on the accepted records as above (no reconcile runs, D2-63) and removes `upgrade`, and then the frozen part of the final check runs again, pure, as after a freeze action (§3.6), so the confirmation and its id, which hashes the end table, are new. A confirmation from before the upgrade is never used for Apply.
  - **In applying:** slice 2's Apply recovery runs first, unchanged (an upgraded session has no `SessionFreezes.end`, and slice 2 never wrote freezes.json). Applied: the session has ended and needs no upgrade. Not applied: it is back in confirming, and is upgraded as above.
- Freeze actions write as §3.6 says; requests are recorded when asked and when answered. Nothing writes freezes.json during a session (ES-7).

### 5.3 The session stack (UN-4, UN-5; 3b)
- A freeze action is one step: `before` and `after` hold, for each key it changes, the key's standing-cleared flag and its own freezes (D3-22, R2-2 of review round 2), with the item changes (each with its `rev`) and taken-out premises of its un-accepts, and the Show results force they ended (`endedForce`). Undo and Redo touch only those keys, so an Undo never changes a key that another action changed, on the stack or not. An Undo whose keys are no longer as its `after` left them is refused as `conflict` (fail closed; it cannot happen while every freeze change is on the stack), and so is one whose un-accepted item has a newer rev (D2-73), exactly as for `decide`; an Undo that re-accepts puts `endedForce` back (ultra review round 1, L8, IMP-12).
- **Back to review** (from `confirming`) keeps the stack as it is: the freeze actions made in confirming stay on it, and in effect. D2-54's "the stack as it was before Submit" still holds for everything else, since going to `confirming` pushes nothing (R2-2).
- **Undo** restores `before` and the items' states through slice 2's checks (RV-5 and the rest). It follows planFreeze's rules, as UN-4 asks (R2-14 of review round 2): an Undo that would give a reason to a target planFreeze would leave now (D3-6's targets' Proof, Dependencies and mark; D3-45; D3-46; a provisional card) is refused with that target's text. The reducer cannot know the queue, the phase or the board, so the runner computes these with planFreeze on the view's board and session flags and passes them as the undo action's `freeze.left` (ultra review round 1, IMP-5, §2.3). It is also refused when `violates` is not empty: "“‹label›” changed since you unfroze it: freeze it again to freeze its new version." The runner computes `violates` with `frozenFindings` on the judged state the view shows (J during a step; the masked state while the results are hidden, RV-6), with the table the Undo would restore. Every key the Undo gives a standing or own reason gets `madeAt` = now (F3 of review round 1): during a step, Claude's work since the Unfreeze is not in J, so a check that then fails on the restored freeze is fresh and not counted, Claude is told in its next tool result, and a give-up rolls back to S₀ (§3.8). That is what freezing again by hand does (UN-4, FZ-10).
- **Redo** sends the request again through the reducer, with values computed then (D2-73): the runner passes the redo action's `freeze.plan` (planFreeze on the recorded request, now), `freeze.values` (the view's judged state, now) and `freeze.left`, and the reducer refuses a freeze action's Redo without them (§2.3). It sets `madeAt` the same way.
- **Seals:** slice 2's, plus `answer` (a question or a request). During a step a freeze action sits above the queue's seal, so its Undo and Redo are allowed; any other action stays refused (D2-62). In confirming, the Undo and Redo of a freeze action with no item changes are allowed too (§4.9).
- Decisions that also change freezes (§3.7) record `freezes` on their `decide` step; Undo restores both. The stack is discarded at the end (UN-6). A5 holds: Ctrl+Z after an accidental "Unfreeze all other cards" restores the freezes, and in a stage the statuses too.

### 5.4 The end (FZ-8, EN-2, EN-3; rows in 3b, writes in 3e1)
- **Rows** (`changes`), whole-state (F14 of review round 1): the end state against the start snapshot. A key frozen at the end (any reason) that the start snapshot does not hold is "keep frozen"; a key the start snapshot holds that has no reason at the end is "keep unfrozen"; any other key has no row. So a standing freeze the person unfroze and froze again is no row. Exit offers keep only where the frozen value is `{ from: 'base' }` (B's text and B's held map, §3.6, R2-1), since Exit leaves the person's files as B; an unfreeze is always offered, but for a foreign record (D3-41). A key with a foreign record that the person unfroze in the session (no reason at the end, whether or not this subject's own file also holds it, since the foreign record would freeze it again anyway) makes an `unfrozen` row whose `keep` is false at Apply and at Exit, with the reason "Frozen on ‹S›'s board, which a session never changes: it is frozen again when the session ends; unfreeze it there to lift it for good." Nothing is written for it: its record never left that board's file, so FZ-8's restore needs no write, and REVIEW 8's list still names it (ultra review round 2, FID-1). A foreign record still in force at the end makes no row.
- **Apply:** `planApply` adds freezes.json: the file as it is on disk, merged by key with the ticked changes (kept freezes added, kept unfreezes removed), minus the records of cards, declarations and arrows F deletes (A6). F already holds every card's written id (the session's `ids` commit, which no revert takes out, D3-37; F5 of review round 1, R2-6 of review round 2), so Apply writes no `id:` line of its own: kept freezes add only freezes.json lines, the facts of F describe exactly what Apply writes, and the backstop runs on F as in slice 2. As a safety net, Apply refuses when a kept freeze names a card whose `id:` is not written in F: "“‹c›” has no written id in the session's files, so its freeze can't be kept: untick it." The file's text as `planApply` read it (`before`) and its planned text (`freezes`) are recorded in `SessionFreezes.end` (`how: 'apply'`) before Apply writes (F18). `applyChanges` accepts freezes.json as a third sidecar, the last change of Apply's change set, so Apply's one Undo step also takes the kept freezes back (D3-32). **Apply's recovery** (slice 2 §3.10 step 7; ultra review round 1, L3) decides applied or not applied by the model files, exactly as slice 2 does, and then makes freezes.json follow: applied, and freezes.json equals `end.before`: telelean writes `end.freezes`; not applied, and freezes.json equals `end.freezes`: telelean writes `end.before` back (deleting the file when it is null); equal to the side it should be, nothing; anything else: a notice naming the kept changes ("freeze or unfreeze them again"). So a crash between the model files and freezes.json never leaves a kept unfreeze in the file of a session that goes back to confirming, nor drops the ticks of one that was applied.
- **The ids at Apply.** Since F holds the ids, an Apply that writes model files also writes the `id:` of every derived-id card of the subject, into every file of the subject that holds one, even files no accepted item touched; that is TL112's write-back, which every board edit of a block already makes, and no difference to any item. The user guide says so (D3-37).
- **An empty Apply (D2-39) with ticks, and Exit with ticks:** no model file changes with the session. telelean makes one board step, "Keep ‹n› freezes from the session": freezes.json merged as above, plus the `id:` of each kept derived-id card in the person's files (D3-16, `planIdWrites`). It goes through `ModelWorkspace.applyChanges` with action `set-freezes` (its changes: the freezes.json merge, built with `parseFreezesFile` and `serialiseFreezes`, and `planIdWrites`' files), and `ExternalChangeSet` gains `snapshot?: boolean`, false when only freezes.json changes, so a History snapshot is made only when an `id:` line is written (FZ-9, D3-21; ultra review round 1, IMP-13). Its ticked changes, the file's text it merged from (`before`), the planned text and the card list are recorded in `SessionFreezes.end` (`how: 'board-step'`, `written: false`) by the run.json write that ends the run.
- **Its order and its replay** (ultra review round 1, L4). The board step runs right after that write, before `end()` resolves and before the run's terminal state unlocks the board: the ES-9 lock and `startRun` wait for it, so no board action and no new session's snapshot can come between. It writes `end.freezes` when freezes.json still equals `end.before`, and otherwise merges `keep` with the file as it then is; then a run.json write sets `written: true`. `recover()`, which otherwise reads only runs that have not ended, also reads the ended session run whose board step is not written (at most one: it makes it before a new session can start; `pruneRunDirs` keeps such a run's directory until then, R2-15 of review round 2) and never merges `keep` again: when freezes.json equals `end.before`, it makes the step (the window closed before it ran); when it equals `end.freezes`, the step counts as done (an `id:` still missing is written, `planIdWrites` being idempotent); anything else (the step ran, its `written` write failed, and the person changed the file since) gives a notice naming the kept changes ("freeze or unfreeze them again"). Either way the run is marked written, so it is not tried at every reload. A board step that fails on the normal path gives the same notice. So a window closed in between loses no kept freeze, and a stale replay never undoes a later board change.
- **A failed or crashed session** writes nothing: the standing freezes are as at the start. Keep ticks never change the final check's id; freeze actions in confirming do, since it hashes the end table (§3.6).

### 5.5 Reload and History
- freezes.json is on disk; session freezes and requests are in run.json, with the running step's `startedAt` and `since` (§3.8), so the rollback, the early end and `fresh` read the same times after a Resume. An open request comes back like an open question and is answered on Resume. Notices not yet sent are lost; the resume turn's FROZEN section replaces them.
- A History restore leaves freezes.json as it is; records that no longer match are listed (A6).
