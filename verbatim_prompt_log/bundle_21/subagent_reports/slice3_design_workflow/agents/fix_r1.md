The main design changes:
- **One subject per session:** steps change only the session's subject, the whole model open included. Other subjects' files keep their text and meaning (D3-41).
- **Two-value rule:** at step purposes, a frozen value counts as broken only if it differs both from its frozen value and from its value at the step's start. This replaces the `inherited` bookkeeping (D3-5).
- **madeAt:** a new per-key `madeAt` time decides which failures are fresh and when a step rolls back to its start, so a freeze restored by Undo during a step counts too.
- **Accepted items:** an accepted item is recorded and checked exactly like your own freeze: its content key plus a held map.
- **Ids:** one `ids` commit at session start writes every derived-id card's id. F then holds every id, and Apply's backstop runs on exactly F (D3-37).
- **Decisions over frozen content:** one text-only check before every decision's write. A decline asks first; un-reject and the Undo or Redo of a decision are refused; an Add premise's Accept waits while its target's proof is frozen; the judge's own reverts become stage problems (D3-48).
- **Whole-model fixes:**
  - Content declined Shaky stays fixed.
  - A step's own delete and proof-broken removals are exempt from "other proofs stay fixed".
  - Every empty, unfrozen Lean statement of the subject may be filled.
  - A new ledger `changed` entry records where a whole-model Change came from, so Submit knows which step reworks it.
  - The new ledger kinds count only while their item's content is in the working copy.
- **New build order:** 3a → 3p (the meaning probe, moved up) → 3b → 3d (3f beside it) → 3e1 (3g beside it) → FROZEN verified on its own → 3c1 → 3e2 → 3c2 → 3e3. "Claude may add cards" comes last, with a fallback to slice 4 logged in advance (D3-43).
