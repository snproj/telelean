# Slice 4 plan: the transform window (spec v4.1)

*Integrator's plan, 2026-11-20, written on autopilot (the user is away and asked for the slices to be built without stopping; `verbatim_prompt_log/bundle_21/user_requested_documents/02_autopilot_decisions.md` logs every choice made without them). The specification is `verbatim_prompt_log/bundle_21/user_requested_documents/01_spec_v4.md`; clause IDs refer to it. The user's own words outrank every simplification here: `bundle_20/prompt20.md` (TRANSFORM 1–10, DECLINE OPTIONS 2, REVIEW 4, EDIT SESSIONS 1, INITIATING 2, FROZEN 3–5) and `bundle_21/prompt21_midturn.md` (★D1: no direct edits during a session; ★D6: search by sentence; message 3: two Undo stacks, a base that never changes). Two designs fed this plan: the transform engine and checks (core, runner) and the transform window (webview, host, protocol); the references design fed §3.12 and D4-28. Every choice the spec leaves open, and every narrowing of it, is in §7 as an "AUTOPILOT DECISION" with its alternative and how to reverse it, cited as (D4-n). Slice 2's plan (`docs/slice2-plan.md`, final, with its §8) and slice 3's plan (`scratchpad/plans/slice3-plan.md`, frozen) are the interface this plan builds on; their decisions are cited as (D2-n) and (D3-n). Ultra review rounds 1 and 2 revised it (§8).*

*Designed for the simplest sound mechanism (AP-24's lesson). A transform is data, a draft. One pure analysis decides what it changes, what it needs and what blocks it; one pure planner writes it. Inside a session a transform is carried out by one queue step of a new kind whose start S₀ is the judged head, so slice 2's step limits, rollback, reconcile and backstop, and slice 3's freeze table and checks, apply unchanged. telelean writes what you typed, records it in its ledger and judges that write before Claude acts, so what your typed Lean means is frozen from then on, and the write must stay as written; it is owned by "typed" items, accepted as yours. Outside a session the window is a provisional session (a real run, so slices 2–3's lock, base, freezes, stack, reload and Exit apply); a transform that needs no Claude ends it and is written as one board step, after a confirmation. Anything that would need repairing is refused with a sentence.*

**Slice 4 = ★D8 (a) item 4, the transform window:**
- **Transform cards…** (a labelled Context Menu button and a right-click entry): with no session, it opens the window in a provisional session, with every other card of the subject frozen (ES-3, INITIATING 2; D3-1 made real); in a stage, on the Proposed view, it opens a window whose transform waits for Submit review (RV-9, ES-9).
- **The window** (TR-1–TR-11): exact copies; new cards (axiom, proposition, vocabulary); a trash you can drag back from; field states (kept as it is, open, "Claude fills this", kept empty; what you type is frozen); Kind with its impact, naming the frozen arrows it removes (G6); Dependencies as card chips found by searching sentences (★D6); Proof kept or cleared; "Claude may add cards"; Instructions; a "Before you submit" panel (TR-13's dependents with their choices, typed-Lean problems, "Claude must define").
- **Submit transform** (TR-15): unchanged → the session goes on with an empty first stage; needs Claude → its first step; changed without Claude → one board Undo step and no session, after a confirmation. In a stage: queued.
- **Decline → Transform** (DO-2, RV-8) for one item or a group, with the previous version alongside; **Ask me instead** becomes one (D2-13 retired); a suggested premise can be transformed (TRANSFORM 9, D4-14).
- **Carrying transforms out at Submit review** (RV-12): one step per transform, telelean's typed write first, then a check or Claude's turn, all or nothing; your typed content is owned by typed items accepted as yours (RV-7 b5); content copied from Claude stays Claude's (TR-15); accepted items your change touches return to review after a confirmation (RV-5 b3).
- **Checks** (TR-12, TR-13, TR-14 a/b, S8–S11): typed Lean is checked (the single-term and shape rules, a scratch elaboration against your files, then what it elaborates to where it is written); what telelean wrote for you must stay as written; nothing is left broken in a session; transform steps write no proofs; your change becomes the frozen value (FZ-3 2e); the `transform` and `session-start` reasons (FZ-7).
- **The rest:** what you typed is listed separately at Apply (EN-2); queueing and dropping are session steps (UN-4); drafts survive a reload (ES-6); the banner "Everything except the selected cards is frozen" (FZ-11).

**Not in slice 4:**

## 2. Contracts (integrator's; builders implement them as written)

### 2.1 Drafts and windows (core `transform/types.ts`, 4a)
```ts
/** `card:<id>` (an axiom or proposition of the subject), `vocab:<fully qualified Lean name>` (a vocabulary item of the
 *  subject), `new:<n>` (a card made in the window; n from `nextNew`, never reused in the draft), `premise:<item id>` (the
 *  Add premise item of a premise window: only `sentence`, `why` and `statement`; no kind, proof or dependencies; never
 *  trashed; D4-14, IMP-8). */
export type DraftKey = string;
/** CF-2's fields (TR-6). Kind, a new vocabulary card's Lean name and Proof are choosers, with no field state. */
export type DraftFieldName = 'sentence' | 'why' | 'stance' | 'source' | 'statement' | 'dependencies' | 'declaration' | 'gloss';
/** TR-4, for this transform only (D4-10): not kept + empty = fuzzy; not kept + filled = open; kept + empty = kept empty;
 *  kept + filled = kept as it is. A value that differs from its original is typed (TR-5): kept when filled, never open
 *  (D4-31); emptied, fuzzy (TR-5) or kept empty. "Kept" is a limit of this transform, never a session freeze (D4-10). */
export interface DraftField<V> { value: V; kept: boolean }
export type FieldState = 'kept' | 'kept-empty' | 'open' | 'fuzzy';
export interface DraftDependencies { sources: DraftKey[]; fromNothing: boolean }
export interface DraftCard {
  key: DraftKey;
  kind?: NodeType;                 // AXIOM | PROPOSITION; absent for vocabulary and premises; chosen, never fuzzy (TR-2, TR-11)
  leanName?: string;               // a new vocabulary card's short name, chosen at creation (TR-2)
  sentence?: DraftField<string>; why?: DraftField<string>;
  stance?: DraftField<Stance | null>; source?: DraftField<Evidential | null>;
  statement?: DraftField<string>;  // the statement's term: the body of `abbrev n : Prop := …`
  /** Propositions and axioms: on an axiom, a typed source or the mark makes it a proposition (D4-36). */
  dependencies?: DraftField<DraftDependencies>;
  proof?: 'keep' | 'clear';        // only on a copied proposition with a proof or a pending claim (TR-4)
  declaration?: DraftField<string>; gloss?: DraftField<string>;   // vocabulary (CF-2)
  trashed: boolean;                // TR-3
}
/** TR-13, per dependent; "Delete it too" moves the dependent into the draft's trash instead (D4-21). */
export type DependentChoice = 'claude' | 'rewrite' | 'broken';
export interface TransformDraft {
  format: 'telelean-transform-draft'; version: 1;
  cards: DraftCard[]; nextNew: number;
  mayAddCards: boolean;            // TR-7 (a), default false
  instructions: string;            // TR-8; plain prose until slice 5
  dependents: Record<string, DependentChoice>;   // by card id or `vocab:<name>`
}
export type TransformOutcomeKind = 'start-session' | 'claude-step' | 'direct' | 'queue' | 'queue-claude' | 'nothing';
export interface TransformProblem { card?: DraftKey; field?: DraftFieldName | 'kind' | 'proof' | 'trash' | 'leanName'; text: string }
export interface TransformDependent {
  card?: string; vocab?: string;
  via: Array<{ kind: 'arrow' | 'proof' | 'statement' | 'definition' | 'reference'; of: DraftKey }>;
  frozen: FreezeReasonView[];      // its reasons but accepted ones (the Submit cascade handles those, D4-21)
  choices: DependentChoice[];      // §3.4
  target?: true;                   // a session target: its Result is reworked, no choice (D4-19)
  /** D4-39: a proof that breaks only if Claude changes Lean the window leaves open or fuzzy (`via[].of`): no choice to make;
   *  an unfrozen one that is no target joins `dependents.proofs`, the others are listed only. */
  mayBreak?: true;
  /** A declaration of another subject that relies on what changes (slice 3's `other-subject` rule): a problem in a session. */
  otherSubject?: string;
}
export interface TransformAnalysis {
  changed: boolean; outcome: TransformOutcomeKind;
  reasons: string[];               // why Claude is needed (TR-15), from TRANSFORM_TEXTS.reasons
  problems: TransformProblem[];    // block Submit transform
  warnings: TransformProblem[];    // TR-13 b2, a trashed source, a typed instance or coercion (§3.4), the touched and cascade previews
  dependents: TransformDependent[];   // D4-39's may-break proofs included
  kinds: Array<{ card: DraftKey; from: NodeType; to: NodeType; impact: KindChangeImpact;
    removed: { arrows: Array<ArrowEnds & { frozen: FreezeReasonView[] }>; mark?: { frozen: FreezeReasonView[] } } }>;   // TR-11, G6
  mustDefine: string[];            // TR-12 (c), from the scratch
  sessionNames: string[];          // names only the session's visible content defines (D4-6)
  touched: string[];               // session: item ids whose content the typed write replaces or removes
  cascade: string[];               // session: accepted item ids that would leave accepted (RV-5 b3, §3.8 step 3; confirmed at Submit review)
  reworkResults: string[];         // session: targets whose Result is reworked in the same queue (D4-19)
  /** Keys of existing cards and vocabulary whose value the typed write changes: `typed`, a field you typed (the `transform`
   *  reason, TR-5); otherwise a follow-on edit (another card's Dependencies, a mark, a kind, a cleared proof, a docstring
   *  mirror), which only revalues the key's reasons (D4-34); a field cleared and left fuzzy is in none (D4-31). New cards'
   *  keys come from planTypedWrite's ids (§3.9). */
```

### 2.5 Protocol (core `protocol.ts`; types and validators in 4a, routing in 4i)
Every message takes `requestId`, is answered by `actionResult`, is in `RUN_MESSAGE_TYPES` (the ES-9 lock never refuses it), and has a hand validator that returns a fresh object, drops unknown fields (`origin`, `provenance` and `consented` never survive, D4-1, D4-27) and refuses `__proto__` keys.

|---|---|---|
| `openTransform` | `{ subject, from, runId?, stage? }`; `from` is `{ kind: 'cards'; cards; vocab }`, `{ kind: 'decline'; items: [{ item, rev }]; instructions? }` or `{ kind: 'edit'; transform }` | cards: ids; vocab: Lean names (`leanName()`); together 1–`LIMITS.transformCards`, distinct; items: 1–`transformCards`, distinct, rev 0–`LIMITS.revision`; instructions ≤ `LIMITS.instructions`, may span lines; transform a token; `runId` and `stage` both or neither, and neither only with `cards` (the provisional start) |
| `saveTransformDraft` | `{ runId, window, rev, draft }` | window a token; rev 0–`LIMITS.revision`; `transformDraft()`; answered with the window's new `analysis` |
| `submitTransform` | `{ runId, window, rev, draft, expect, confirmed? }` | as save; `expect` a `TransformOutcomeKind`; `confirmed` keeps only `outcome` and `cascade` (`true`) and `direct: { keep }` (`keep` ≤ `listLength` freeze keys, distinct) |
| `cancelTransform` | `{ runId, window }` | tokens; a stage's window only (a provisional window's Cancel is `endSession exit`, D4-2) |
| `dropTransform` | `{ runId, stage, transform, confirmed? }` | token; `confirmed` a boolean |
| `submitReview` + | `confirmed?: { transforms: true }` | only that key |
| Retry step's message + | `confirmed?: { transforms: true }` (§3.9 step 7) | only that key |

`transformDraft()` checks only the draft's shape (IMP-28): format and version exact; cards 1–`LIMITS.draftCards`, keys `card:‹id›`, `vocab:‹leanName›`, `new:‹0–9999›` or `premise:‹item id›`, distinct; a vocabulary card (a `vocab:` key, or a `new:` key with `leanName` and no `kind`) has only `declaration`, `gloss`, `trashed` and, when new, `leanName` (a Lean identifier ≤ 200 characters); an axiom or proposition card has `kind` and only CF-2 fields; a premise card has only `sentence`, `why` and `statement`, never `trashed: true`; sentence, why and gloss one line ≤ `textLength`; stance and source their enums or null; statement and declaration ≤ `LIMITS.leanText`, may span lines, no NUL; dependencies' sources ≤ `listLength`, distinct, never the card itself, never with `fromNothing`; proof `keep` or `clear`; `nextNew` above every `new:` key; instructions ≤ `LIMITS.instructions`; dependents ≤ `listLength` entries, each a `DependentChoice`. ## 3. Design

### 3.1 The provisional session (ES-3, INITIATING 2, EDIT SESSIONS 1; D4-2)
- **Opening.** "Transform cards…" with no session posts `openTransform { subject, from: cards }`: Current view; at least one card or vocabulary item of the board's subject; no provisional card. The host applies first the board edits that reached it (slice 1e's order), refuses while any run of the folder is active (`busyRefusal`), then calls the runner. The runner makes, in its first run.json write:
  - the run, its shadow and its `base` commit;
  - the session at phase `transform`, stage 0, `claudeStarted: false`, with slice 3's freeze snapshot and `start.transform` = the selection (§3.11);
  - the window record, from `draftFromModel` on the board's model (= B), with its analysis.

  RunState is `review`, so `runLocksBoard` locks every board of the folder (ES-9). No consent, no Claude, no Lean.
- **In phase `transform`** the runner reads B as the board's ProjectModel and facts (ModelWorkspace, whose files equal the base commit; drift refuses Submit), so nothing is built. Freeze actions work (FZ-9), with values `{ from: 'base' }`, since the working copy is B.
- **A session with no inference** (a transform-started one, until a Formalize; IMP-4): its `CheckContext.selection` is `{ subject, target: '', premises: [], adoptedGhosts: [] }`, which the engine's `validateContext` accepts at session purposes (4h); `RunView.target` is `''` and `premises` `[]`, and `PersistedRun.selection` likewise (4i).
- **Submit transform** (outcome by §3.2):
  - `start-session` (nothing changed, nothing for Claude: TR-1): the runner runs `checkBase` first, writing base-facts.json ("Your files must build first: ‹errors›"; the window stays, IMP-24); then one write clears `window` and opens stage 1, empty and `judging` (the view shows B until then); then a judge of the head (= B) publishes its judgement. Formalize can run from it. `recover()` of a stage with no `judged` runs that judge, after `checkBase` when base-facts.json is missing (LC-17).
  - `claude-step` (TR-15): the host asks the egress consent (once per root, as today) and passes `consented: true`. The runner runs preflight, the backend and `checkBase` ("Your files must build first: ‹errors›"; the window stays), opens the conversation, and makes one `queue-start` write (SealBy `transform`) that clears `window`, adds the QueuedTransform (from `cards`, no items, no action), sets `claudeStarted` and the conversation id, and queues its step (§3.9; LC-17, IMP-18). Stage 1 opens when it ends; when the step does not pass, its transform stays queued with `lastFailure`, and stage 1 opens empty with Retry step and Exit session (ES-4, D4-4).
  - `direct` (changed, nothing for Claude): §3.6, after its confirmation.
  - In every context, an outcome that differs from `expect` answers `needs-confirmation` (`confirm: 'outcome'`, §4.2's dialog); a `claude-step` sent without `consented` answers `needs-confirmation` (`consent`), which the host answers by asking the egress consent (IMP-18); a `queue-claude` needs none until Submit review (§3.8 step 6).
- **Cancel is Exit** (EN-2's exception). `endSession exit` is sent at once when no freeze action was made since the window opened; otherwise slice 3's Exit dialog opens, led by "Cancel the transform? The edit session it started ends; your files are not changed. Your ‹n› freeze changes in this session are dropped, except those you keep." A provisional session that ends writes no attempt record and is dismissed (no ended-run line).
- **Reload** (ES-6): the window comes back from run.json (§5.3).

### 3.2 Field states, changes, the outcome and the problems (TR-1, TR-4, TR-5, TR-15)
- **States are the transform's own** (D4-10, D4-31; F3, UI-5, UI-11). Each field's chip shows its effective state:
  - **kept**: "kept as it is (this transform)", or, for a value you typed, "frozen: you typed it" (it becomes the `transform` reason when carried out);
  - **kept empty**: "left empty (this transform)", or, for a field you cleared, "frozen empty: you cleared it";
  - **open**: "open: Claude may change it"; **fuzzy**: "Claude fills this";
  - a field the session freezes (any reason, `sessionFrozen`): "frozen in this session (‹reasons›)", with the rose lock; its chip offers neither open nor fuzzy ("Frozen in this session (‹reasons›): unfreeze it first.").

  Typing a value that differs from the original sets kept, and a typed non-empty value cannot be set open ("You typed it: it stays frozen. Put back the copy, or clear it, to let Claude change it.", D4-31); clearing a field whose original was not empty makes it fuzzy (TR-5), and the chip can make it kept empty; a field edited back to its original value takes its original state back (UI-11). The window's Context Menu Freeze and Unfreeze are real session freeze actions (§4.2) that also set the chip: Freeze sets kept or kept empty; Unfreeze removes the session's reasons and sets an untyped field open or fuzzy; on a field kept only by its chip, Unfreeze is a draft edit that sets it open or fuzzy ("Opens it for this transform: it is not frozen in this session."); on a value you typed it is disabled with D4-31's text. Only real freezes are called "frozen" (D4-10).
- **Changed** (TR-1 b): a field value that differs from `original` (Dependencies compared entry by entry), a new or trashed card, a Kind change, a cleared proof. State changes alone never count.
- **Needs Claude** (TR-15), any of: a fuzzy field (a slot included); instructions not blank; "Claude may add cards"; an open Lean statement on a card whose sentence changed; a typed statement naming something not defined (`mustDefine`); a dependent left to Claude. Each gives a reason (`TRANSFORM_TEXTS.reasons`). D4-39's may-break proofs are no reason of their own: the open or fuzzy Lean they come from already is.
- **Outcome.** Start context: needs Claude → `claude-step`; else changed → `direct`; else `start-session`. Stage context: needs Claude → `queue-claude`; else changed → `queue`; else `nothing` (the window closes; a decline window's items stay in review, TR-9 b). In an edit window, `nothing` answers `needs-confirmation` (`confirm: 'drop'`): "Nothing is left to change: drop “‹label›”?", and confirming sends `drop-transform` as one step (LC-14). A premise window never needs Claude: it offers no fuzzy field, instructions or tick (§4.2, D4-14).
- **Problems** block Submit:
  - every TR-12 (a) failure; the TR-12 (b) and (c) problems of §3.3 (the scratch's own, a typed piece using a name this draft deletes, R2-7, an unknown name in a declaration or in a premise window, R2-10); a cycle over drawn, solid and draft arrows (AR-3);
  - a dependent with no choice; a dependent left to Claude that is frozen by a reason other than accepted, and, in a session, a dependent of another subject (§3.4); a fuzzy field the session freezes: "You asked Claude to fill it, but it is frozen in this session (‹reasons›): unfreeze it, or type it." (a fail-safe: the chip refuses it, above);
  - a new vocabulary name that is not an identifier or clashes; a kept empty sentence on a card that is not new;
  - a new card with a typed field but a fuzzy sentence: "Type the sentence of a new card whose other fields you fill, or clear them to let Claude make the whole card." (D4-15); a typed arrow from or into a slot; a new vocabulary card with a typed gloss but a fuzzy declaration, since telelean has no declaration to put the gloss on: "Type the declaration of a new vocabulary card whose gloss you write, or leave both to Claude." (D4-15, R2-8);
  - an axiom made a proposition with neither a typed source nor the mark: "An axiom becomes a proposition only through what it follows from: add an arrow into it, or mark it Follows from nothing." (D4-36);
  - a changed Lean statement on a card whose proof is kept: "“‹c›”'s proof proves its old statement: clear it (prove it again after the session, with Formalize for dependencies)." with [Clear the proof] (D4-20, D4-34); not on a session target whose proof is its Result's, which is reworked instead (§3.4, IMP-9);
  - §3.4's refusals for kinds and session targets.

### 3.3 TR-12: typed Lean (D4-6, D4-7, D4-32)
- **(a)** `typedLeanProblems` runs live in the window; at Submit, on every typed field before anything else, and the runner never starts a Lean job while it finds anything (S4M-11); and again in `planTypedWrite` and in the direct planner (fail closed).
- **(b)** At Submit transform the runner calls `ModelWorkspace.checkTypedLean` (4g): one `lake env lean --stdin --json` at the model root, under the refresh's sandbox, `runExclusive` and the memory guard.
  - It is refused while the board's facts are stale ("Wait for the Lean check of your files, then submit.") or the files differ from the base ("Your files changed since the session started: they must match it again before telelean checks your Lean.").
  - The source: the library's imports; `set_option autoImplicit false` and `set_option relaxedAutoImplicit false`; `namespace ‹S›.telelean_typed`; the typed vocabulary declarations and statements ordered by mentions, what a piece uses first (a cycle is a problem), each statement as `abbrev ‹its statement's short name› : Prop := (‹text›)` (brackets balance by (a), so a second command cannot parse; S4M-11); the ghost check's in-file probe (`gate/scratch.ts`), printing each piece's elaborated constants; `end`; and a final `#print "telelean-typed-end"`, whose message must appear. The transform's own names shadow your files' inside the namespace. The typed write writes new vocabulary in the same order (§3.5).
  - Messages are attributed by line. An "unknown identifier" or "unknown constant", in a statement or a declaration, goes to `unknown`; any other error on a typed line is a problem on its field. An error on no typed line, or a missing end message, refuses the check, fail closed: "telelean could not check your Lean: ‹first error›." (IMP-13).
  - **Visibility** (S4M-10, IMP-13). A piece's elaborated constants must be visible where the typed write puts it: from a module its destination imports (the subject's vocabulary module for vocabulary, Inbox for a new card's statement, its own module for an existing card's), from its destination above the place it is written, or a typed piece written before it there. Otherwise a problem on its field: "“‹c›” uses ‹name›, which ‹module› cannot see where telelean writes it." Residual (D4-6): a typed change to a structure or inductive is checked as a second copy in the scratch namespace, so a statement that needs its new parts is refused.
  - **Deleted names** (R2-7). The scratch imports your files as they are, so a declaration this draft deletes (a trashed card's statement or `.holds`, a trashed vocabulary declaration) still elaborates there, and the dependents walk reads the view's facts, where the typed text does not exist yet. So a typed piece whose `consts` include such a name is a problem on its field: "“‹c›” uses ‹name›, which this transform deletes." `analyseTransform` checks it at every analysis, from the scratch's `consts` and the draft's trash, so a card trashed after the check is caught too. With visibility, this is why the direct path never writes Lean the scratch passed but its place cannot build; a piece without `consts` never takes the direct path (it needs Claude, or is carried out by a step whose build catches it).
  - **Over sorts** (IMP-27): a typed `axiom` passes the shape rule on its elaborated type, against the model's sorts and the typed ones.
  - **Proofs** (R2-11; TR-14, D4-37): the probe also reports whether each typed declaration's elaborated type is a proposition, and one whose type is (core `isProof`'s rule: a Prop-valued def, abbrev, instance or opaque) is a problem on its field: "“‹name›” is a proof (its type is a proposition), and a transform writes no proofs (TR-14): type a definition, or leave the proof to Formalize for dependencies." And `no-proofs`' exemption for typed units (§2.8) never covers a typed declaration that `isProof` finds, so one the scratch could not elaborate (it uses a session name) fails its step instead; no proof is ever written as yours.
  - `consts` keeps the constants of each piece that elaborated with no error and no unknown name.
- **(c)** An unknown name that the session's visible content defines (the view's vocabulary and card statements: `sessionNames`), or that a typed declaration of the same draft declares, is fine in a statement and in a declaration alike (S4M-9), and checked when the transform is carried out (D4-6). The others are `mustDefine` in a statement, shown as "Claude must define: ‹names›", and make the transform need Claude; in a declaration they are a problem: "Define ‹name› first: a typed declaration may use only what exists or what this session shows." telelean still writes the statement itself; Claude defines the names (D4-7). A piece with an unknown name has no `consts`: what it means was not confirmed by your check, so its typed item takes its own Accept (D4-32). A premise window never needs Claude (D4-14), so there an unknown name the session does not show is a problem, not `mustDefine`: "A suggested premise's Lean may use only what exists or what this session shows." (R2-10).

### 3.4 TR-13, TR-11 and session targets (D4-13, D4-19, D4-20, D4-21, D4-39)
- **Dependents** of each trashed or changed card or declaration, cleared proof and removed arrow, from the view's facts (masked while the results are hidden, RV-6 b4), inside the window or outside it:
  - **proof:** a card whose proof uses a removed or changed `.holds` (`usersOf`, `axiomListUsers`, transitively through the proofs that go too) or a changed declaration (`uses` reach), or uses a removed arrow's source (direct(v));
  - **statement / definition:** a card statement or vocabulary declaration whose meaning closure reaches a changed or removed declaration (slice 2's `reach`);
  - **arrow:** arrows out of a trashed card, removed with it as follow-on edits (TR-15); listed, with no choice, unless a proof uses one (that proof is then a proof dependent);
  - **another subject's** (S4M-16, IMP-22): a declaration of another subject that reaches a changed or removed declaration (slice 3's `other-subject` rule), with no choice: in a session a problem, "“‹x›” of ‹S2› relies on it: change it outside a session."; outside one, listed;
  - **reference:** none until slice 5 (§3.12).
- **May break** (D4-39, S4M-6). For each Lean statement or vocabulary declaration the window leaves open or fuzzy, the proofs that would break if Claude changed it: the card's own kept proof, the proofs that use its `.holds` (`usersOf`, `axiomListUsers`) or reach the declaration (`uses` reach). Each is listed as "may break if Claude changes ‹x›", with no choice to make: an unfrozen one that is no session target joins `dependents.proofs`, so Claude may mark it broken if it breaks (TR-14), and it can be deleted too; a frozen one, or a session target's Result, is listed only ("frozen: if Claude changes ‹x›, its step cannot pass" · "“‹T›”'s result uses ‹x›: Claude cannot change ‹x› without breaking it"), and Claude's turn names it as fixed.
- **Instances** (S4M-16): in a session, a typed new instance, `class … extends` or coercion whose class frozen or accepted content uses is a warning: "An instance can change what frozen statements mean; Submit review refuses it then." (its step's frozen check does, fail closed; outside a session freezes bind Claude only, FZ-1).
- **Choices.**
  - "Delete it too" moves the dependent into the window's trash; its own dependents join the list. Restoring it from the trash removes it from the draft, and it returns to the list with no choice (LC-18).
  - `claude` when the dependent has no reason but accepted ones (accepted items leave accepted through the Submit cascade, §3.8). For a proof in a session it reads "Mark its proof broken (Claude writes no proofs; re-prove it after the session)" (F14): it becomes a Proof broken item (TR-14 b); for a statement or definition, "Leave it to Claude".
  - `rewrite` (D-11, only for a P→A of a conditional proof) and `broken` (today's Delete anyway) only with a `direct` outcome. With them the outcome cannot need Claude: "With Claude's step nothing is left broken: choose Delete it too or Leave it to Claude for “‹c›”."
  - A dependent left to Claude that is frozen by a reason other than accepted is a problem: "“‹c›” relies on ‹x› and is frozen (‹reasons›): unfreeze it to leave it to Claude, or delete it too.", with [Unfreeze them] (slice 3's Unfreeze on those cards: every reason goes, an accepted one returning its item to review with RV-5's list, which TW-1 allows). With a `direct` outcome a frozen dependent needs no Unfreeze (FZ-1: freezes bind Claude only): Delete it too, Rewrite or Leave it broken, as for the board's Delete, the provisional session's session-start freezes included (F15, IMP-10).
- **TR-13 b2.** A changed sentence whose Lean statement stays unchanged and kept is a warning: "You changed the sentence of “‹c›”, but its Lean statement stays as it was: the two may disagree. Let Claude redo its Lean statement, or clear it." with [Let Claude redo its Lean statement] (a chip change: the statement becomes open, and its may-break proofs show) and [Clear it] (UI-5). Its typed item gets c3.
- **TR-11 with G6.** Each Kind change shows `kindChangeImpact` on the view's graph, with the incoming arrows and the mark it removes named by their source's sentence, each frozen one with its reasons. A→P happens only through the axiom's Dependencies (D4-36): a typed source or the mark switches its Kind, which keeps the axiom as pending (AR-6, with its glow-loss sentence); P→A removes the incoming arrows and the mark and replaces the proof by `axiom n.holds` (AR-7). In a session, a P→A whose conditional proof has dependents is refused: "Move it to axioms after the session: telelean would have to rewrite proofs that use it." (D4-13).
- **Session targets** (cards with a Result in the session; D4-19):
  - Kind, Dependencies and Proof are locked: "“‹T›” is a target of this session: its arrows are its question, and its proof is its Result's.";
  - trashing one, or a card with an arrow into one (the trash would change that target's question or Dependencies), is refused: "“‹c›” has an arrow into “‹T›”, a target of this session: trash it after the session.";
  - its statement may change only when B has no proof of it; its Result is then reworked in the same queue (`reworkResults`), and D4-20's kept-proof problem does not apply, since its proof in the working copy is its Result's (IMP-9);
  - a change to anything B's proof of a target uses is refused: "“‹T›”'s proof in your files uses ‹x›: change ‹x› after the session.";
  - a target whose current proof (its Result's content) uses what changes joins `reworkResults` too, computed once the results are shown (Submit review).

### 3.5 The typed write (`planTypedWrite`, 4c)
One composition on EditSession, in this order. Each piece is recorded as a TypedUnit with the exact value written; `owner` is `typed:<card>` or `typed:vocab:<name>` (a `premise` unit's is its Add premise item, step 10, R2-10), and the follow-on removal of a trashed card's arrows is owned by that card's typed item; a unit of no field you typed (a follow-on edit's, a trash's, a new block's bookkeeping lines) is marked `follow` (§2.2, R2-1). `keys` lists every freeze key whose value the plan changes, `typed: true` for a field you typed and `false` for a follow-on edit (another card's Dependencies, a mark, a kind, a cleared proof, a docstring mirror; D4-34), with its pending text; a cleared field's soft unit is in none (D4-31), while the soft docstring mirror keeps its follow-on entry (step 3, R2-5). D-11's spans (step 5) were computed on the unedited text: each is mapped through `EditSession.mapOffset`, and the plan refuses (`unsafe`) when an earlier edit replaced it (IMP-23).
1. **New cards** (not slots): a block in the subject's Inbox module as `add-node` writes it, with an explicit id from `newCardId` (`deriveId`; taken: B, the working copy, the ledger's cards, the ids the freeze records name (D3-54) and the draft's other ids; TR-2 c), the typed fields, and no `worded_by`. The block's existence is its `block` unit, and each line written (`id:`, `type:`, `natural_name:`, `asserted:` and the typed fields) its own `line` unit (S4M-3). A typed statement goes in an abbrev after the block, with its `lean:` pin, plus `axiom n.holds : n` for an AXIOM, each its own unit.
2. **Vocabulary:** new declarations at the end of the subject's vocabulary module namespace, in the scratch's order (what a declaration uses first, §3.3); a changed one by `replace-decl`, its docstring kept; a gloss by `set-docstring`. A declaration you typed is a `decl` unit without its docstring, and a gloss you typed a `docstring` unit, so declaration and gloss are yours or Claude's apart, as slice 3's values are: a typed gloss leaves an open declaration to Claude, and a typed declaration an open gloss (R2-5).
3. **Field lines** by `setField`: sentence, Why, stance, source. A typed sentence on a `worded_by: agent` block drops that line (CF-6, G5), a unit of its own. It updates the statement's D-9 mirror only when the docstring equals the old sentence, the declaration holds no Claude content (D4-29), and the window keeps the Lean statement: one the window leaves open or fuzzy is Claude's to rewrite whole, so its mirror is left alone (S4M-7). A mirror's change is a soft `docstring` unit with a follow-on `keys` entry: it revalues the statement's reasons (D4-34) but need not hold, so a later transform that opens that statement (TR-13 b2's remedy), a whole-model Formalize or an answered `request_unfreeze` can still change it (R2-5). When the statement is typed too, its `decl` unit carries the new docstring, and no `docstring` unit is written.
4. **Statements of existing cards:** `replace-decl` of the abbrev, its docstring kept; or, for a card without one, an insert with its pin (and a `.holds` axiom for an AXIOM).
5. **Kinds:** P→A through `planKind` (`holds-theorem-to-axiom` or `add-holds-axiom`; the incoming entries and the mark removed); A→P (through a typed source or the mark, D4-36) sets `type:` and keeps the axiom as pending.
6. **Dependencies:** `premises:` entries added and removed per the draft's diff; the mark set or removed (never together with entries, B.6).
7. **Cleared proofs:** `remove-holds`, archived in direct mode: unproved, never pending (TR-4).
8. **Trash** (TR-3 b): `delete-node`'s pieces (the block, its statement, its `.holds`, archived in direct mode, and the entries naming it in other cards' `premises:`), or `remove-decl` for a vocabulary card. Its `block` unit (`present: false`) supersedes every earlier unit on that card (its lines, its own `premises:` entries, its bound declarations and their docstrings), and a vocabulary card's `decl` null its earlier `docstring` units (§2.2), so a card whose fields an earlier transform typed, or that it made, can be trashed (R2-4).
9. **Cleared fields left fuzzy** (session mode): the field emptied (its line removed; for a Lean statement, its abbrev, pin and an AXIOM's `.holds`), each a soft unit (D4-31): yours if Claude leaves it so.
10. **A premise** (a premise window): its typed values in one `premise` unit, owned by the Add premise item, with no `keys` entry; no file is written (D4-14, R2-10).
- It never edits a line or declaration that holds Claude content, except to replace it whole: a line, a declaration's body, or its docstring (D4-29, R2-5). `touched` lists the items whose content a unit replaces or removes (session mode), an earlier typed item among them when a unit of this plan supersedes one of its units (§3.8 step 3, R2-1). Slots and the `dependents` choices are not written. Any `problems` (a LeanOp that could not apply, a span an earlier edit replaced, a cycle) refuse the whole plan.

### 3.6 The direct path (ES-3, TR-15 outside a session; D4-3, D4-16)
1. In phase `transform`, `submitTransform` with outcome `direct` runs the analysis and TR-12 (b) on B. Without `confirmed.direct` it answers `needs-confirmation` (`confirm: 'direct'`), and the webview opens the confirmation (§4.6), whatever freezes changed: a session ends here (EN-2, REVIEW 8; F1, UI-7). It lists the changes (as links), the fields you typed that become standing freezes (D4-16), and each freeze change made in the provisional session (slice 3's rows, with `keep.apply`'s ticks). With `confirmed.direct`, a dry run of `planAction(apply-transform)` on the board's model with exactly the planner's inputs (pure). Any problem refuses, and the provisional session stays. Drift refuses: "Your files changed since you opened the window: Cancel, then open it again."
2. The runner ends the provisional session (discarded, dismissed, no attempt record) and answers `{ ok: true, transform: { outcome: 'direct' } }` with the board action, its `originalKey` and the kept rows as `keep`. The host holds the folder busy from `submitTransform` until its board step is done, so no other run starts in between (LC-4).
3. The host performs `apply-transform` first in its board queue: one history entry "Transform: ‹label›", one board Undo step, `graph` and `workspace` posted before `actionResult`. If it is refused (a hand edit in that instant), nothing is written, and the toast reads "Your transform was not applied: ‹reason›. Its window is closed." (D4-3's residual, which also names a crash or reload in that instant).
- **`planAction(apply-transform)`** (`edit/planTransform.ts`):
  - recomputes `original` with `draftFromModel(model, keys)` and refuses when its `originalKeyOf` is not `originalKey` ("Your files changed since you submitted the transform: open it again."), so a hand edit is never written over (LC-4, S4M-15), while the field states, which follow the provisional session's freezes, never count (§2.6, R2-6); then refuses `invalid` on any problem of `analyseTransform` (context `start`, outcome `direct`, `frozen: {}`: freezes bind Claude only, so your own edit never needs an Unfreeze, FZ-1; F15, IMP-10) or when `reasons` is not empty ("This transform needs Claude: submit it from the window.");
  - then `planTypedWrite` in direct mode, plus D-11's rewrite for dependents marked `rewrite`, and nothing for `broken` (left as they are, as Delete anyway leaves them);
  - D3-44's standing freezes for the typed fields of existing and new cards and vocabulary (sentence, Why, stance, source, statement, declaration, gloss), each storing its cards' sentences, with the derived `id:` of each card it freezes written first (D3-16, D3-54; D4-16, F11); and `keep`'s rows: a kept freeze added, a kept unfreeze's standing record removed (F1);
  - A6's drops for trashed cards and removed arrows and marks; the stored positions of trashed cards dropped (new propositions are placed by the board);
  - its label.

### 3.7 Windows in a stage (RV-9, ES-9 b3, DO-2, RV-8, RV-10; D4-8, D4-14, D4-18)
- **Opening** (`openTransform { runId, stage, from }`): in phase `stage` only, from the Proposed view or the review list. It is refused while a step runs or is queued (D2-62's text), while judging ("Re-checking the working copy: transform in a moment."), while another window is open, in confirming ("Back to review first."), from the Current view ("During an edit session, Transform cards… works on the Proposed view: switch to Proposed."), and for a card that a queued transform holds ("“‹c›” is in the queued transform “‹label›”: edit that one instead."). The window record (a records-only write) copies the stage judgement's state, masked while the results are hidden (`masked` lists those fields); `basis` is the judged commit and `viewVersion`. Its analysis is computed with it, and again after every save and every freeze action, whose write also brings the record's source revs up to date (TW-1, R2-9).
- **The runner's window checks**, on every save and submit (IMP-28): a `card:`/`vocab:` key outside the window's keys only when trashed ("Delete it too"); `proof` only on a card that has one; a premise window's limits (below).
- **Decline → Transform** (DO-2), from the detail's Decline ▾, the D menu's T, a group's Decline… and the Context Menu's review-item entry.
  - Items that take it: Formalize, Change, Add, Delete, Vocabulary term, Declaration, typed, and an Add premise on its own (D4-14, D4-26).
  - A group takes the items that fit and names the others (`not-transformable`, IMP-26) with their reasons: arrow items and Results ("Transform applies to cards: decline arrows and results on their own."); Proof broken ("A transform writes no proofs: accept it, or reject it."); a premise in a group ("Transform a suggested premise on its own."). A hidden Result's part is never listed.
  - The window shows each item's card as in the working copy, with "Previous version" (B's) beside it; a Delete item's card starts in the trash; Claude's values start open, marked "Claude's proposal" (D4-9).
- **Ask me instead** (RV-10 b2): a decline window on the row's items that take Transform, with the instructions "Ask me about: ‹choice›" prefilled. When none takes it, D2-13's Shaky decline stays (D4-26).
- **A premise window** (D4-14) edits only the premise card's sentence, Why and Lean statement (key `premise:<item id>`); its target is shown read-only, and it offers no fuzzy field, instructions, tick, new card or trash (UI-18): emptying a field reads "Type it: telelean carries out a suggested premise's transform without Claude." It never needs Claude, so an unknown name in its Lean statement is a problem (§3.3 (c), R2-10). At Submit review a no-Claude transform step carries it out: its `premise` unit, owned by the premise item and making no typed item (no typed units hold check, no "accepted as yours"; R2-10), rewrites the draft in the session's records (a typed sentence sets `wordedBy: 'user'`) in the run.json write that ends the step as passed, so a step that does not pass leaves the draft as Claude proposed it (LC-9). The queue-end judge checks it again (slice 2 §3.8: one that no longer closes the gap is to be redone), and it returns to review "updated". Its Accept still asks how you hold it.
- **Submit transform** recomputes the analysis on the stage judgement's state, with TR-12 (b) (kept as the queued transform's `scratch`), then sends `queue-transform` through the reducer: one session step, "Queue transform: ‹label›" (an edit window's is `replace`, "Edit transform: ‹label›").
  - The window closes and the transform is queued.
  - A decline window's items become declined `transform`, Shaky-like: their content stays; their dependents in review become to be redone, not reverted; accepted dependents need RV-5's confirmation first. The step records what it changed, as decide's does (§5.1).
  - An outcome that differs from `expect` answers `needs-confirmation` with it (usually TR-12 (c)'s names; §3.1). `nothing` closes the window and changes nothing, but in an edit window, where it asks to drop the transform (§3.2).
- **While queued** (D4-8, D4-43):
  - a source item's status changes only by dropping or editing its transform. Its own decisions are refused ("It is part of a queued transform: drop the transform to bring back its ‹n› items."); a decision whose cascade would reach a source (a Reject's, a plain decline's or a Shaky decline's, whose cascades take in what is declined Shaky-like) answers `needs-confirmation` instead: "This also takes out “‹c›”, which your queued transform “‹label›” holds: drop the transform with it?". Confirming (`dropTransforms`) drops the transform in the same recorded action, and its Undo restores both (LC-6). A judge's revert that reaches a source marks its transform stale ("“‹label›” lost “‹c›”: edit or drop it.");
  - `decide` with option `transform` is refused: "Decline with Transform opens the transform window.";
  - a mid-stage Formalize refuses a target with a Transform-declined item, as for Shaky (RV-9 b1, D2-53): "“‹T›” has a transform waiting for Submit review: formalize it after."; slice 3's whole-model limits keep content declined Transform fixed.
- **Drop** (`drop-transform`, "Drop transform: ‹label›"): its sources whose content is in ws/ return to review, as a Shaky item does; those a judge's revert took out stay to be redone, their decline dropped (D4-43). It also takes the transform's step out of `retry` (LC-8). Drop never asks: it is one Undo step. An Undo that would give a source back a status its content no longer has (the queue's Undo, a drop's Undo) is refused: "“‹c›” was taken out since: drop the transform, or queue it again with Transform cards…."
- **Edit…** reopens it (`edit` mode), rebuilt by `rebaseDraft` (LC-1): `previous`, `locked` and the copy come from `draftFromModel` on the current state (unmasked once the results are shown); `original` is the new copy; a draft field equal to its old original (you never typed it) takes the new copy's value, state and provenance; a field you typed keeps your value, owner `you`; a card that is gone goes to `left` and leaves the draft. Provenance is never recomputed by comparing the draft with the new copy, so a value Claude wrote becomes yours only when you type it. Cancel leaves it queued; Submit `replace`s it, taking the old transform's step out of `retry` too.
- **Cancel** of a stage window changes nothing (TR-9 b).
- **Un-reject** of an Add that a transform trashed is refused: "You deleted “‹c›” in “‹label›”: add it again with Transform cards…." (LC-15).

### 3.8 Submit review with transforms (RV-12, RV-5 b3, D2-57, D3-49; D4-5, D4-30, D4-40, D4-42)
1. **Gates:** slice 2's (nothing in review, the results shown, no step, not judging), plus no window open.
2. **Each queued transform is analysed again** by the runner, on the real state (the results are shown), with its stored `scratch` (`mustDefine` less the names the working copy now defines; IMP-11); `planSubmit` reads these analyses (`analyses`). It is **stale** (`transform.stale`, D4-30) when:
   - a card it names is gone, a copied value in the working copy differs from its `original` (but the fields copied from a mask, its `masked`: a target's Proof, Dependencies and mark, which `reworkResults` handles; LC-7), or a problem appears;
   - a dependent needing a choice, or its needs-Claude reasons, changed;
   - a target in `reworkResults` has its Proof frozen by a reason other than accepted ("“‹T›”'s proof is frozen and uses ‹x›: unfreeze it, or drop the transform.");
   - its typed Lean names a declaration owned by an item this Submit reworks (declined Shaky or to be redone): "“‹label›” uses ‹x›, which Claude reworks at Submit review: accept or reject ‹x› first, or edit the transform." (D4-42, S4M-12);
   - a judge's revert reached one of its sources (§3.7).

   Text: "“‹label›” needs a new look: open it with Edit…". Submit review's refusal names each stale transform, whose row in Queued transforms shows the text with [Edit…] (UI-16). New targets in `reworkResults` need no choice.
3. **Cascade** (RV-5 b3; S4M-5): the accepted items each transform touches (an earlier typed item among them when a unit of the transform supersedes one of its units, since a typed item covers only the fields you typed, §3.5, §3.10, R2-1); the accepted items whose covered targets (`acceptCovers`, RV-4) include a key of its `typedKeys` (typed or follow-on: an accepted Formalize covers its card's sentence, so retyping that sentence cascades it); the accepted items whose `frozen.held` has a key whose value it changes or removes; the accepted Results in `reworkResults`; and their accepted RV-5 dependents. When it is not empty and `confirmed.transforms` is absent, the answer is `needs-confirmation`, "These accepted items return to review, because your transforms change what they rest on: …" (slice 2's dialog). Cancelling leaves everything queued. So no accepted expectation is left on a key a transform step changes: those are never revalued (§3.11).
4. **`planSubmit`** gains `analyses` and `transformSteps: Array<{ transform: string; rework: boolean; claude: boolean }>`, in this order: the queued transforms that need no Claude, then those that do, each group in queue order (RV-12 b1, D4-40); then one rework step per carried-out transform whose steps made Shaky or to-be-redone items (D4-24); then slice 2's Submit steps, with the inferences of `reworkResults` among the affected ones. The take-out (D2-57) also takes out `reworkResults` (to be redone, reverted), under D3-49's keep set (the `transform` and `session-start` reasons count as frozen).
5. **Writes** (LC-11): the cascade (in review, `frozen` cleared, slice 3's R2-4) is part of the `submit-take-out` write, one recorded write that also records `PersistedSession.submitting`; then `queue-start` with its seal (D4-5), which clears `submitting`. `recover()` that finds `submitting` with no queue started makes the `queue-start` write, then waits for Resume, as for slice 2's AF3. Even a queue of no-Claude transforms seals and ends in stage + 1, never in confirming.
6. **Consent:** in a session whose conversation has not started (`claudeStarted` false: only a transform-started one, LC-10) and whose queue has a Claude step, the host asks the egress consent first; the runner opens the conversation, with the context bundle, before that step (D4-27).
7. **A transform that is not carried out** (its step did not pass): the stage report lists the accepted items that returned to review for it: "‹n› items returned to review for “‹label›”, which was not carried out." (LC-11).

### 3.9 The transform step (runner `run/step.ts`; core judge; D4-4, D4-7, D4-23, D4-25, D4-33)
1. **Plan** (IMP-12): `planTypedWrite` (pure) in session mode on the head's model, before anything is recorded, so its `ids` give new cards their ids for the scope and the freezes. A plan with problems fails the step before it starts (its transform stays queued with `lastFailure`). A rework step has no plan.
2. **Start:** commit `step-start` (S₀, the judged head), with the StepScope from `transformScopeOf(t, a, sessionFrozen, plan.ids)` (`open` minus every key the session freezes, so a freeze wins; new cards' keys through the ids) in the same run.json write.
3. **Typed write** (a recorded write, LC-5, IMP-20): one run.json write with the ledger `typed` entry and one TransformFreeze per `plan.keys` entry (pending, with its `typed` flag, §3.11); the files into ws/; commit `typed` (a new shadow kind: S₀ᵗ); then a run.json write setting the step's `typedCommit`. `recover()` takes a step without `typedCommit` back to its start (§5.3). A rework step skips this.
4. **Typed judgement** (D4-23, S4M-1), before any Claude turn: `engine.check` on the `typed` commit, with a build (purpose `gate-other`, the transform scope, `typedStart` the judged model itself, R2-2; a no-Claude step's check already was this):
   - each TransformFreeze takes its full value (text, meaningKey, held map) from it when it has a meaningKey for every declaration the freeze covers; otherwise it stays pending (LC-16). The held map leaves out what the window leaves open or fuzzy, the dependents left to Claude and the MUST DEFINE names: changes to those come back as items the typed item depends on (RV-5). From here the frozen and held checks compare what your typed Lean means;
   - **typed meaning:** each typed statement or declaration with scratch `consts` (your check confirmed it) must elaborate here to those constants, the scratch namespace's names mapped to the names written. Otherwise the step fails at once, uncounted, and is taken back (D4-4): "“‹c›”'s Lean statement elaborates differently in the working copy than when you checked it: ‹names›." (code `transform`, token `typed-meaning`). A coercion, an instance or a shadowing name already in the working copy is caught here; one Claude adds during its turn changes the meaningKey the freeze now holds, so the frozen check refuses it (cheats T11, T12);
   - a typed piece without `consts`, or without facts here (a module holding a MUST DEFINE name does not build), goes into the entry's `unconfirmed`, in the same run.json write as the values (D4-32).

   Its other findings fail a no-Claude step (step 5) and are Claude's to fix in a Claude step (step 6).
5. **No Claude:** the typed judgement is the step's check. With no step finding the step ends `passed`; otherwise it fails: "Your transform “‹label›” could not be carried out: ‹findings›."
6. **Claude:** the turn (§2.8). Claude works from the `typed` commit with the transform's tools. Normalise (core `gate/normalise.ts`, IMP-5; the runner passes it the scope and the live entries) pins the statements of `open`, new cards' included (as slice 2 pins `fuzzyStatements`), writes the step's ledger arrows and `card` blocks (with their kind and slot), and writes `worded_by: agent` for a sentence Claude changed, compared with the `typed` commit. Gate attempts (`gate-other`: no target, no comparator; up to 3) judge every rule of slices 2 and 3 with the transform scope. A transform step is computeItems' `active` step, with `transform` (§2.9, R2-3): typed ownership first, then its declarations new since S₀ are statement-phase `vocab:` and `decl:` items with it as origin, never proof material, and it makes no Result draft, so a MUST DEFINE name, plain vocabulary and a definition the tick allows are owned at every attempt, never `unowned`. It is no inference's active step, so `stepFindings` are the global findings and the step-purpose rules (IMP-4):
   - **step limits** against S₀: every declaration and block line keeps its text and meaning, except the step's own typed units, the `open` fields, the statements and definitions of `dependents` left to Claude, `mark_proof_broken`'s removals, the cards `add_card` made, and new declarations: plain vocabulary (the shape rule), definitions of the MUST DEFINE names, and, with `mayAddCards`, other definitions, instances, coercions and presuppositions (items like any; without it `transform`, `new-definition`, D4-35);
   - **frozen and held** (slice 3); a key with a TransformFreeze of the running step's transform expects that value for every reason the revalue covers (§3.11), never excused by S₀'s value (the two-value rule's S₀ is before the typed write); accepted expectations are never revalued, and the Submit cascade cleared them first (§3.8 step 3, S4M-5);
   - **typed units hold** (D4-33, S4M-2): the newest live unit of every `typed` entry of the running queue (whose typed items reconcile makes at its end), and of every earlier entry whose typed item is in review or accepted, has exactly its value in the state: a `decl` unit with `null` means no declaration of that name, bound or not, and otherwise its declaration's text without the docstring (a typed card statement: with it); a `docstring` unit its declaration's docstring; a `line` unit its line, `worded_by` included; an `entry` unit its entry; a `block` unit the block's existence. Superseded units read as §2.2 says (a trashed card's earlier units as absent, R2-4). Exempt: soft units; units of fields you typed whose `transform` reason the person removed (an Unfreeze, which also returns their accepted typed item to review, §3.10); and `premise` units, which no file holds and the premise item owns (R2-10). A `follow` unit is never released by an Unfreeze (R2-1). Code `frozen`, token `typed`; never excused by S₀; at `stage`, a problem, and at `final` and in the backstop, a refusal;
   - **S₀ᵗ** (D4-33): for every line and name a typed unit covers, G5, B.4, new-axiom and `no-proofs` compare with the `typed` commit's version (`JudgeInput.typedStart`: S₀ plus the live typed units), not S₀'s;
   - **S₀ᵗ's items** (R2-2; slice 2's frozen-items rule, A-V2B2-ADV-4): in a step that made a typed write, the rule that every item of S₀ outside `openItems` keeps its content key reads the items of the `typed` commit (`typedStart`), not S₀'s, since the write itself replaces or removes items (`touched`: a Claude value you typed over, a Claude Add you trashed, Claude's arrows out of a trashed card, an earlier typed item); its open set is `openItems` plus every item of S₀ᵗ that owns content (slice 3 §3.7's item ownership) under a key in `TransformScope.open`, under the Dependencies of an `arrowsInto` card, or under a `dependents` statement, definition or proof. So a no-Claude step's typed judgement, which judges the `typed` commit itself, finds nothing here, and Claude may revise an open field that Claude's own item holds, while a change to any other item of S₀ᵗ still fails (`existing-changed`, token `session`). A rework step keeps S₀ and its `openItems`;
   - **G5:** Claude changes no `type:`, `follows_from:`, `premises:` (telelean's ledger writes) or `worded_by` (normalise's, CF-6); in a new block of yours, no line appears but Claude's fields of `open` keys and normalise's pins (S4M-3);
   - **B.3** with §2.8's arrows, the live typed `entry` units counting as the person's; **B.4** (no `.holds` changes kind but by a typed unit); **new-axiom** (D2-40 included; allowance 1 asks a `.holds` to be still bound to its card, so an axiom a trashed card bound counts as new, D4-33); **`transform` (`no-proofs`)**; **stray-command**; **`rejected-repeat`**, with DO-3's identical set per transform in a rework step;
   - every inference is judged, and other inferences' findings never count against Claude (RV-11).

   `finish` says `done` or `cannot`; `cannot` passes when the gate passes, and its gap is reported (A3). The state must build: whatever the transform broke is fixed, marked broken or deleted (TR-13: nothing is left broken).
7. **All or nothing** (D4-4). A step that fails, gives up, is cancelled or hits a cap goes back to `step-start`, with its ledger entries and TransformFreezes dropped (only its own: an earlier transform's on the same key is current again, LC-3). Its transform stays queued, with `lastFailure`. **Retry step** of a transform step (LC-8) first runs §3.8 steps 2–3 for its transform (stale: refused with the stale text; a cascade: slice 2's dialog, `confirmed`), and scopes the step from the transform as queued now (its mode, consent and scope again, with a new plan and typed write); it refuses a step whose transform is no longer queued. A failure ends the queue as slice 2 says (the rest not run).
8. **Step end:** ledger `created` (phase `statements`: a transform step has no freeze_statements, so nothing it makes is ever a Result's part; computeItems owns such a declaration through its step, which `transformSteps` maps to `transform:<id>`, as a `vocab:` or `decl:` item whose origin is the transform, IMP-4, R2-3; a Claude step's entries also name each new declaration of yours on which it wrote a docstring, so that part has an owner, R2-5) and slice 3's `changed` entries; the transform moves from `queued` to `done`; a premise step's `premise` unit rewrites its PremiseDraft in this same write (LC-9). Transform steps write no AttemptRecord: their outcomes are in the stage reports (LC-19).
9. **Rework step** (D4-24): the done transform's scope narrowed to its `openItems` (`open`, `arrowsInto` and `dependents` only what those items own; no slots; `mayAddCards` false; S4M-14), `rework: true`, no plan and no typed write; its feedback turn is slice 2's for those items; D2-15 holds (D4-12).

### 3.10 Ownership, typed items and the queue end (4d; D4-1, D4-22, D4-32, D4-33, D4-44)
- **`computeItems`** (S4M-3). Typed ownership comes first. A difference B→W is owned by a typed item when the newest live typed unit covering it has exactly W's value: a block line, a `premises:` entry, a declaration's text without its docstring (a typed card statement: the whole abbrev), a docstring, or a block's existence (a trashed card's: its absence). Live means in a `typed` entry that no rollback dropped; a later unit supersedes an earlier one of the same identity, and a block's or a declaration's absence supersedes the earlier units on it (§2.2, R2-4); `premise` units own no file content (R2-10). A declaration's docstring and the rest of it are owned apart once a unit covers either (R2-5): a part whose newest unit has exactly W's value is typed, and the other part, or one that differs from its unit, belongs to the item slices 2–3 give that declaration (`decl:<n>` for an existing one; for a new one, the item of the step whose `created` entry names it), whose content key then covers only that part. In a new block of yours, every line and bound declaration that differs from its unit, or has none, belongs to `card:<c>` (Formalize for the statement, its pin and an AXIOM's `.holds`; Change for field lines), and a ledger arrow into or out of it to its `arrow:` item; each of those depends on the typed item (below). Every other difference keeps the rules of slices 2–3. A running transform step is the `active` step, with `transform` (§3.9 step 6), and after it its `created` and `changed` entries resolve through `transformSteps`, as `vocab:` and `decl:` items with the transform as origin and no Result (IMP-4, R2-3). So typed and Claude content of one card are owned line by line, and a value Claude writes is never yours unless you typed exactly it.
- **Typed items** (`typed:<card>`, `typed:vocab:<name>`; one per card across the session's transforms, carried beside RV-1's two, D4-1). Labels (F16): "Change ‹card› · you typed it", "Add ‹card› · you typed it", "Delete ‹card› · you typed it", "Change vocabulary ‹name› · you typed it".
  - Its content key covers each of its units that must hold (not the soft ones, nor those of fields you typed that your Unfreeze released; `follow` units always), with the unit's value in the state, so a unit that must hold and does not changes it (D4-33).
  - **What its Accept freezes** (R2-1, D4-44): `acceptCovers` of a typed draft is the freeze keys of its units of fields you typed (neither soft nor `follow`), with no RV-4 pairing: never Claude's statement beside your sentence, which would freeze it at a value the typed item does not own, never a follow-on key (D4-34), and for a trash nothing, as RV-4's Delete. Its TransformFreezes already hold those keys; the accepted reason makes an Unfreeze of one of them return the item to review (FZ-7), as its content key then leaves that unit out.
  - Classes for the badges only: no own Accept (RV-7 b5) unless it is unconfirmed (D4-32), untouched by D2-23, exempt from the backstop's `own-accept` and `class` (an unconfirmed one must have its own Accept).
  - Never meaning items for the reveal: you typed their content, and they cannot mention proof material (`other-result`).
  - RV-5 edges as for any item, with rule 3 extended (S4M-4): a typed item depends on the item that adds the card any of its units sits on, and on the items adding either end of a typed entry. So it stays in review, typed-by-you, until that Add is accepted, and the Add's Reject lists it in RV-5's confirmation and withdraws it.
  - RV-4's pairing as an edge (R2-1, D4-44): a Formalize or Change `card:<c>` that changes c's Lean statement depends on `typed:<c>` while `typed:<c>` owns c's sentence (never an Add, which `typed:<c>` depends on instead, so no cycle). So Claude's formalisation of a sentence you typed waits for yours (accepted as yours at the queue end), and the Reject or withdrawal of your sentence lists it in RV-5's confirmation, to be redone.
  - Decline options Reject (B's version of its units back, through `planItemRevert`) and Transform; no Shaky ("Claude may not rework what you typed.").
  - Its Reject, and a cascade of the person's action that withdraws it, remove the TransformFreezes on its units' keys in the same recorded action (the decide step's `freezes`, LC-2); un-reject and Undo restore them; `frozenChangesOf` leaves them out (S4M-13, IMP-15).
  - A cascade that takes one out makes it withdrawn, never to be redone; un-reject of what took it out brings it back in review. A judge's revert removes no reason (slice 3's invariant), so a typed item it takes out leaves a frozen stage problem, which the person's Unfreeze resolves, fail closed.
- **The queue end** (`reconcile` with `queueSteps`):
  1. `heldAtJudge` and `coverAtJudge` for every draft (slice 3, R2-1). Then each typed draft of this queue's typed entries, and each earlier typed draft one of whose units they superseded, is created or updated (a `premise` unit makes none, R2-10), and **accepted as yours** (`how: 'yours'`, `frozen` from its `heldAtJudge` and `coverAtJudge`) when none of its pieces is in `unconfirmed` and each RV-5 dependency outside the typed set is accepted, as a fixpoint over the queue's typed drafts; a dependency the results hide counts as not accepted (slice 2's "waits for the results to be shown"), so the outcome never reads a hidden status. Otherwise it is in review: marked `typed-by-you` ("you typed it: accept it once the ‹n› items it uses are accepted"), taking a plain Accept; or, unconfirmed, marked `typed-unconfirmed` ("you typed it, but telelean could not compare what its Lean means with your check: read it in its fully explicit form, and accept it on its own"), taking its own Accept (D4-32).
  2. Each passed transform's sources return to review, "updated" when their key changed (slice 2's F5 rule, generalised); withdrawn, `replaced-by-transform`, when no draft is left. A trashed Add of Claude's is declined (Reject), so it is never proposed again; its un-reject is refused (§3.7).
  3. A record named in a typed entry's `touched` that has no draft is withdrawn, `replaced-by-transform` (not slice 2's "disappeared" problem); one with a changed key follows slice 2's rule ("updated").
  4. Pending TransformFreezes take their values from this judgement when it has a meaningKey for every declaration each covers; the others stay pending, and the next judge-apply with keys values them, as slice 3's `upgrade` does (AP30-4; LC-16). The judge-apply runs `frozenFindings` again after setting them (slice 3 §3.5).
- **What an accepted item holds where its content does not reach** (R2-1, D4-44; amends slice 3 §3.3, whose "the others keep B's value" a typed key never meets). RV-4 pairs targets an item's content need not own: a Formalize's or Change's sentence, an Add's fields that typed lines (or other items) own. Each such target of an accepted item now expects its value in the judgement the Accept read, "the content you accepted" (FZ-3): judgeState fills `coverAtAccept` for every draft beside `freezeAtAccept`, reconcile copies it into the record's `coverAtJudge` (set and removed with `heldAtJudge`, slice 3's R3-1 rule), and the Accept, its own or a group's, and the queue end's "accepted as yours" copy it into `frozen.covers`; `effectiveFreezes` gives each entry `{ from: 'cover', item, value }`. A record accepted before slice 4 has no `covers` and keeps B's value. So accepting Claude's reformalisation after you retyped the sentence (TR-15's needs-Claude case), or Claude's Add after you retyped its Why (S4M-4's flow), freezes your sentence or Why at your value, which its TransformFreeze also expects, and Apply stays reachable. An Unfreeze of such a target returns the item to review, as for any covered target; rejecting your sentence returns Claude's formalisation with it (the edge above), and any other accepted item whose `covers` a decline would change is listed by `frozenChangesOf` in that decline's dialog, as a held or frozen row with the item as its holder (slice 3 §3.7; only the declined typed item's own TransformFreezes are left out, S4M-13).
- **`planItemRevert`** of a typed item restores B's version of exactly its units' lines, entries, declarations, docstrings and blocks.
- **A proof a transform cleared** (D4-34, D4-20): a mid-stage Formalize refuses a target whose proof a carried-out transform of this session cleared (`FormalizeSession.clearedProofs`): "“‹T›”'s proof was cleared by your transform “‹label›”: prove it again after the session (Apply, then Formalize for dependencies), or reject your change to bring the proof back." The removal is a typed unit that must hold (D4-33).
- **Backstop:** typed ownership is re-derived from B, F and the live ledger; a typed item must be accepted (an unconfirmed one by its own Accept), with its key on F equal to its accepted key, and every unit of it that must hold holds on F (D4-33); `type:`, `follows_from:` and `worded_by` changes, a `.holds` theorem→axiom, and arrows pass only with their typed unit; a new `.holds` passes only with its typed unit, besides slice 2's allowances (an AXIOM card's `.holds` whose statement is new in the session, which Claude writes when it formalises an AXIOM card in a transform step, IMP-14); a transform's `card` entry is an Add, never an intermediate (the `kind` rule, the mask and `intermediateInferences` skip it).

### 3.11 Freezes (4e; D3-1, D4-10, D4-17, D4-23, D4-34)
- **Session-start** (FZ-7 r2; D3-1 made real). In a session with `start.transform`, `effectiveFreezes` gives every card and vocabulary item of the subject outside the selection a `session-start` entry, whole (every field, its Dependencies and its mark), expected at B, except the keys in `startCleared`. Cards created later are not covered.
  - Unfreeze (of one key, or "Unfreeze all other cards", which counts them with the standing ones) adds the keys to `startCleared` in its freeze delta; Undo restores them.
  - They end with the session (FZ-8), with no keep rows. EN-2's line reads "Started with Transform cards…: the other ‹N› cards of ‹subject›, frozen for this session, return to how they were." The banner "Everything except the selected cards is frozen" shows while one remains (FZ-11 5a); while a Formalize with the whole model open runs in such a session, it reads "Whole model open, but everything except the selected cards is still frozen" (F13).
- **Transform** (FZ-7 r4, TR-5; D4-23, D4-34, S4M-8). Each `plan.keys` entry gets a TransformFreeze with the typed write. A field you typed (`typed: true`) gives its key the `transform` reason ("frozen: you typed it"); a follow-on edit (another card's Dependencies, a mark, a kind, a cleared proof, a docstring mirror) freezes nothing new and only revalues the reasons the key already has, while D4-33 keeps the write itself in place (but the docstring mirror, a soft unit, R2-5). A value is pending (its text) until a judgement with keys values it (§3.9 step 4, §3.10 step 4). Per key, the newest TransformFreeze gives the expected value (a later transform's supersedes an earlier one's; a rollback drops only its own step's, LC-3), and the `transform` reason lasts while one `typed` TransformFreeze remains. They end by the person's Unfreeze in any form (slice 3's decline dialog's `unfreeze` included), by the Reject or person-made withdrawal of their typed item (§3.10), or at the session's end; each such action records them in its FreezeDelta, so its Undo restores them (LC-2, IMP-6). Rows "frozen: you typed it", with keep ticks at Apply.
- **Revalue** (FZ-3 2e). Accepted items your change touches leave accepted through the Submit cascade (§3.8), which clears their `frozen`; an item accepted after the transform holds the value you accepted on what it covers (`frozen.covers`, §3.10, R2-1), which on a typed key is your typed value, so it agrees with the TransformFreeze. The holders of a declaration you change are TR-13 dependents (§3.4): accepted ones cascade, the others must be unfrozen or deleted, so no held value needs revaluing.
- **madeAt** (LC-2): set whenever a freeze action, its Undo or its Redo gives a key any reason (standing, own, session-start, transform), so a reason an Undo restores during a step is fresh (FZ-10) and its give-up rolls back to S₀ (slice 3's F3). The typed write sets none: a transform step goes back to S₀ anyway (D4-4).
- **Slice 3's decline dialog** (its §3.7): `unfreeze` also removes the listed keys' TransformFreezes (recorded); `yours` is not offered when a listed key has one.
- **Views:** the reasons `session-start` ("frozen: the session started with Transform cards…") and `transform` ("frozen: you typed it"); an accepted typed item's own reason on a field you typed is shown once, as "frozen: you typed it" (R2-1); `mode.transform` for the banner. `cardFreezeLook` leaves session-start reasons out of `whole`, the frost, the card's lock badge and the closed field lock, so the banner is their board mark (FZ-11 5a: "rather than putting an aura on every card"); FieldLock's tooltip and aria-label still name "frozen: the session started with Transform cards…", and Unfreeze stays enabled (UI-6). ## 5. Undo and persistence

### 5.1 The session stack (UN-4, UN-5)
- `queue-transform` (queue or replace) and `drop-transform` are one step each, recording the queue entry before and after, every item change with its rev, the step ids taken out of `retry`, and, as decide's step does, the written premises its cascade took out, the force it ended or started, and its commits (LC-13, IMP-7). Undo and Redo go through the reducer's checks (D2-73): undoing a queue restores the sources' statuses and removes the entry; undoing a drop puts both back; each restores ws/, removes a force the action started and restores one it ended; Redo sends `after` again. An item whose rev changed gives `conflict`; an Undo that would give a source back a status its content no longer has is refused (D4-43).
- A decision that dropped transforms with D4-43's confirmation records them (`drops`); its Undo restores them with its other effects. Its `freezes` delta records the TransformFreezes a typed item's Reject or withdrawal removed (LC-2).
- Opening, saving and cancelling a window are not steps. The window's freeze actions are slice 3's freeze steps, whose FreezeDelta records `startCleared` and the key's TransformFreezes (an Unfreeze's Undo restores them, LC-2, IMP-6). The window's draft edits have their own timeline, never a session step (D4-41, §4.5). Text fields keep their own undo (UN-4 4b).
- Submit review with transforms seals (D4-5); a provisional session's stack is discarded with it (UN-6).

### 5.2 The board stack (UN-2)
- `apply-transform` is one board step: Undo restores the files, the freezes.json delta and layout.json, as for any plan; History keeps its snapshot (AR-7: a replaced proof stays in History).

### 5.3 run.json, webview state, reload (ES-6)
- run.json v2 gains, additively: `PersistedSession.transform` (§2.2), `claudeStarted`, `submitting`, `PersistedStep.kind` `transform` (with `transform`, `rework?` and `typedCommit?`), the ledger `typed` entry, the item records' `coverAtJudge` and `frozen.covers` (R2-1), and `SessionFreezes.start.transform`, `startCleared` and `transform`. `recover()` reads absent fields as empty, but an absent `claudeStarted` as true (a session from before slice 4 started its conversation; LC-10, IMP-19); no migration.
- The webview autosaves the draft 400 ms after the last edit, one save in flight, coalesced, and at once on Cancel and `visibilitychange`; a Submit waits for the save in flight, then sends with its rev (UI-9). It keeps a crash copy in its state (`updateState({ transformDraft: { runId, window, rev, draft } })`, debounced 150 ms). When `session` arrives, a window the store lacks opens with the host's draft, or with the crash copy when it has the same runId and window and a rev ≥ the host's (then saved again); a window the host closed closes. The window's draft-edit timeline is not kept across a reload (D4-41).
- **An interrupted transform step** (LC-5, IMP-20). One without `typedCommit` is taken back to its `step-start`: `recover()` reverts ws/, drops its `typed` entry and TransformFreezes, and the step waits for Resume, which runs it from its start (the plan and the typed write again). One with it: a no-Claude step's Resume runs its check again; a Claude step whose first turn was never sent is started, not resumed; otherwise slice 2's Resume (`--resume` and the resume turn). No Resume needs a conversation for a step that has none. Cancel step takes the whole transform back (D4-4). A `submitting` without its queue is finished as §3.8 step 5 says.

### 5.4 Reversal hazard
Reversing slice 4 while a session holds a window, a queued transform or a typed entry leaves run.json fields the older code refuses: finish or Exit such sessions first. The AP entry says so.
